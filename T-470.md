## Cause

Named 28/09/2026 (`/diagnosing-bugs`). The audit caught the commit button part-way through a transition. Its resting colours pass.
- Choosing "Make Test person a Viewer" updates the member list before the server answers. The picked role then equals the listed role, so the button disables and fades to opacity 0.5 over 150 ms. The server refuses, the list rolls back, and the button is re-enabled and fades from 0.5 back to 1.
- The alert waits only for the list's refetch, and the spec at the failing commit (`6f73173`, test at line 401) audited as soon as the alert and the "Admin" pill showed.
- Evidence: with the button's opacity pinned in a live sheet, axe reports exactly CI's reading at 0.5 (#e4e4e5 on #959698, 2.32:1), 2.59 at 0.55, 3.92 at 0.7, and passes from 0.8. Axe skips disabled buttons, so the button was enabled at 0.5, which happens only on the first frame after re-enabling. A per-frame recorder under 6x CPU throttling showed the enabled button at 0.5 for 340 ms with no frame drawn. The sheet is gone by the test's end, so the failing audit was the explicit one inside the test.
- Why only sometimes: the refusal must be slow enough (about 150 ms) for the fade to settle at 0.5, and the runner loaded enough that no frame is drawn between re-enabling and axe's read. The original test could not be made to fail on demand (20 of 20 read opacity 1), so the deterministic red is the gate spec's new fade test.
- Fix, in the gate (`apps/web/e2e/browser.ts`): each audit first waits for every running animation with a finite end to finish; an endless one, such as a spinner, is audited running. `accessibility-gate.spec.ts` gains "audits a button once its fade from disabled has ended" (red on the old gate at 3.38:1, green now) and "audits beside a spinner that never stops turning". The last-admin test's own `toHaveCSS("opacity", "1")` wait, added after the failing run, goes, since the gate now does that job for every spec.
- `--repeat-each 30`: 30 passed, without the spec's own opacity wait. Whole local suite: 194 passed, 10 failed; the 10 are the claude.ai-redirect tests in `consent.spec.ts` and `console-people.spec.ts`, which fail the same way on unchanged `main` in the cloud container. The merge group's `full-web` was green.
- Other specs read for the same hazard (an audit right after an act that toggles `disabled` on a filled button), all now covered by the gate: `display-name.spec.ts:134`, `sign-in.spec.ts:189`, `sources.spec.ts:908` and `:986` (Widen's `disabled={!wider}`), and any audit after the bind act's `disabled={bind.isPending}`. Buttons using `aria-disabled` have no fade (the kit fades only `:disabled`), so `sign-in.spec.ts:314` and `:388` are not at risk.
