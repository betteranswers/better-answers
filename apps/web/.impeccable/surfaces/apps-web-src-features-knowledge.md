# Knowledge area (S3): concept page first

Scope: the Knowledge area of the web app (Search, concept page, Guides, Knowledge Base, All Knowledge, Kinds, Collections and Owners, Exports). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: Bid Writer reuses a concept with confidence; Knowledge Curator keeps it checked; Founder-Operator sees where knowledge is weak; Occasional Expert finds and checks one thing from a cold start.

Constraints: trust words are a closed set, always text; UK dates; sentence case; one page title per page; concepts are any OKF kind, never one content shape.

Chosen direction: Issued Drawing (the incumbent blueprint, sharpened). Memorable moment: the title block beside every concept.
