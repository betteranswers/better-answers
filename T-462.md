## Goal

Where the session-ended line sits beside a **Sign in again** button or link, it says what happened and leaves the action to the control. Where no such control follows, it still says what to do.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] On the auth screens, an unauthenticated refusal reads "Your session has ended." above the **Sign in again** button, with no second "Sign in again".
- [x] In the console, each refusal shown beside the **Sign in again** link does not repeat the link's label. That includes the console-specific wordings that end in or begin with "Sign in again". Each says what happened and, where the words carry it, what signing in brings back (e.g. that the person comes back to this page).
- [x] Any place the unauthenticated refusal shows with no sign-in control after it still ends with the instruction to sign in again.
- [x] The sign-in and console specs read the line from the SPA's tables. The ones that typed the **Sign in again** label as a literal read it from the component's label instead.
- [x] The accessibility gate is green on each state.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Drop the refusal's `next` sentence wherever a sign-in control follows it; keep it where none does.

**Current behavior (verified on main 9fdf00b7, 27/09/2026):**
The SPA's shared refusal words give `unauthenticated` as `{ why: "Your session has ended.", next: "Sign in again." }`, rendered as one sentence by `sentenceOf` / `RefusalLine`.
- The auth screen forces that template for every unauthenticated refusal, then renders the `SignInAgain` button.
- The console's words reuse the same template beside its `SignInAgain` link, which three console views use (revoke everywhere, the names waiting list, correcting a display name).
- The console's own refusal words add two more that start "Sign in again, and you come back …".
- The shared template is also the fallback wherever no control follows, so blanking `next` globally would leave those places with no instruction.

**Desired behavior:**
A refusal rendered next to a sign-in control shows only its `why`, and any words about what is kept. A refusal rendered alone keeps `why` + `next`. The choice is made where the control is rendered, not by editing the shared class words.

**Key interfaces:**
- The refusal words' `{ why, next }` shape and `sentenceOf`. Either a way to render the `why` alone, or per-site words.
- The auth screen's `SignInAgain` button and the console's `SignInAgain` link.

**Out of scope:**
- The sign-in screen's arrival line "Your session has ended.", which already says only what happened.
- The api's server-rendered pages.
- The People feature's refusal words, unless a sign-in control follows them. Check before touching.
- Renaming the button or link.
