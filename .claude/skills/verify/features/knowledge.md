# Knowledge

Search the workspace's concepts, read one, read a guide, browse the concepts by collection and kind, name a collection's owner.

Setup, once, in a fresh workspace, then the recipes in order. `<annualLeaveId>` is the part of the *Annual leave* concept's `iri` after `/c/`.

1. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /workspaces '{"name":"Verify Knowledge"}'` answers `workspaceId`, `admin.id` and `admin.email`.
2. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /people '{"displayName":"Ifan Morgan"}'` answers Ifan's `id` and `email`. Then `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /members '{"workspaceId":"<workspaceId>","userId":"<ifanId>","role":"Editor"}'` answers `{"added":true}`.
3. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /concepts '{"workspaceId":"<workspaceId>","userId":"<admin.id>","concepts":[{"title":"Annual leave","collection":"policies","kind":"Policy","body":"Everyone takes 25 days of leave."},{"title":"Expense claims","collection":"policies","kind":"Policy","body":"Claims go in within 30 days."},{"title":"Fire drills","collection":"safety","kind":"Procedure","body":"A fire drill runs each quarter."}]}'` answers each concept's `iri`.
4. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/sign-in.sh <port> <admin.email>` lands on `/people/members`.
