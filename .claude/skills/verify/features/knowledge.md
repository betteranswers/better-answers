# Knowledge

Search the workspace's concepts, read one, browse them by collection and kind, name a collection's owner.

Setup, once, in a fresh workspace, then the recipes in order. `<annualLeaveId>` is the part of the *Annual leave* concept's `iri` after `/c/`.

1. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /workspaces '{"name":"Verify Knowledge"}'` answers `workspaceId`, `admin.id` and `admin.email`.
2. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /people '{"displayName":"Ifan Morgan"}'` answers Ifan's `id` and `email`. Then `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /members '{"workspaceId":"<workspaceId>","userId":"<ifanId>","role":"Editor"}'` answers `{"added":true}`.
3. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/harness.sh <port> POST /concepts '{"workspaceId":"<workspaceId>","userId":"<admin.id>","concepts":[{"title":"Annual leave","collection":"policies","kind":"Policy","body":"Everyone takes 25 days of leave."},{"title":"Expense claims","collection":"policies","kind":"Policy","body":"Claims go in within 30 days."},{"title":"Fire drills","collection":"safety","kind":"Procedure","body":"A fire drill runs each quarter."}]}'` answers each concept's `iri`.
4. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/sign-in.sh <port> <admin.email>` lands on `/people/members`.

## knowledge-search at `/knowledge/search`

1. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "2 members"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role link click --name "Knowledge" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --fn "location.pathname === '/knowledge/search'"`. Expect the searchbox *Search by a word or a question*.
2. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role searchbox fill "leave" --name "Search by a word or a question"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --fn "new URL(location.href).searchParams.get('knowledge.search') === 'leave'"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Annual leave"`. Expect the status *Showing matches for “leave”.* and the list *Matches* to hold the link *Annual leave*.
3. Second view: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> reload`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Annual leave"`. Expect the searchbox to hold `leave` and the match listed.

## knowledge-concept at `/knowledge/search/<annualLeaveId>`

1. On the Search page after step 2 above, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role link click --name "Annual leave" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --fn "location.pathname.startsWith('/knowledge/search/')"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Everyone takes 25 days of leave."`. Expect the article *Annual leave* with the link *Back to Search* and the regions *Sources*, *Verification* and *Links*.
2. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role link click --name "Back to Search"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --fn "location.pathname === '/knowledge/search'"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Showing matches for"`. Expect the status *Showing matches for “leave”.* and the match listed.
3. Second view: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> open http://localhost:<port>/knowledge/search/<annualLeaveId>`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Everyone takes 25 days of leave."`.

## knowledge-concept-editor at `/knowledge/search/<annualLeaveId>`

1. As the Admin on the concept page, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Edit" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Edit This Concept"`. Expect the button *Edit* inside the concept's header beside the title *Annual leave*, then the region *Edit This Concept* holding the textboxes *Title*, *Kind*, *Description*, *Tags* and *Body*, the group *Sources*, the warning *Saving changes this concept for everyone who reads it.* and the buttons *Save* and *Close editor*.
2. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role textbox fill "Twenty-five days a year." --name "Description" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Save" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Saved."`. Expect the status *Saved.* and the description under the title.
3. Second view: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> reload`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Twenty-five days a year."`. Expect the description to stay.
4. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> press e`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Edit This Concept"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role textbox fill "Twenty-five days." --name "Description" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role link click --name "Back to Search"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "You have changes that are not saved."`. Expect the buttons *Leave* and *Keep editing*, and the page still on the concept. Then `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Keep editing" --exact` and `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Close editor" --exact`.
5. As Ifan, who owns nothing: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/sign-in.sh <port> <ifan.email>`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> open http://localhost:<port>/knowledge/search/<annualLeaveId>`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Twenty-five days a year."`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Suggest an edit" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Suggest an Edit"`. Expect the warning *Saving sends your edit to the concept's owner. Nothing changes until they decide.* and no draft of the Admin's in the Description.
6. `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role textbox fill "Ifan's note." --name "Description" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> press Control+Enter`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Your suggestion is waiting for the concept's owner to decide."`. Second view: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> reload`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Twenty-five days a year."`. Expect the description to stay the Admin's. Then `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/sign-in.sh <port> <admin.email>`.

## knowledge-new-concept at `/knowledge/search`

1. As the Admin, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> open "http://localhost:<port>/knowledge/search?knowledge.search=Cyber%20Essentials%20Plus%20certificate"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "No results found"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Type it as a new concept" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "New Concept"`. Expect the region *New Concept* with *Title* holding `Cyber Essentials Plus certificate`, the picker *Collection* at *Choose a collection*, and *Save* disabled beside *Choose a collection before you save.*.
2. Type before choosing a collection: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role textbox fill "Policy" --name "Kind" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role textbox fill "Cyber Essentials Plus covers the whole company." --name "Body" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> snapshot -i` to read the ref of the combobox *Collection*, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> select <ref> policies`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Saving adds this concept for everyone who can read policies."`. Expect no *You have changes that are not saved.*, *Kind* still holding `Policy`, and *Save* enabled. For Ifan the warning reads *Saving sends your new concept to the collection's owner or an Admin. Nothing is added until they decide.*, and after *Save* the status reads *Your suggestion is waiting for the collection's owner or an Admin to decide.*.
3. Close keeps the draft: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Close editor" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "Type it as a new concept"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> find role button click --name "Type it as a new concept" --exact`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "New Concept"`. Expect *Kind* to hold `Policy` and *Body* to hold `Cyber Essentials Plus covers the whole company.` again, with no ask on Close.
4. Second view: `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> open "http://localhost:<port>/knowledge/search?knowledge.new=Cyber%20Essentials%20Plus%20certificate&knowledge.collection=policies&knowledge.kind=certification&knowledge.tags=cyber-essentials"`, `.claude/skills/verify/scripts/browse.sh <port> wait --text "New Concept"`. Expect *Kind* to hold `certification`, *Tags* to hold `cyber-essentials` and *Collection* to hold `policies`.
