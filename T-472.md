## Cause

Named 28/09/2026 (`/diagnosing-bugs`). The same Radix hand-back T-471 names, reaching a dialog's focus trap.
- The keystroke list is a non-modal Radix popover that hands focus back to its button in a `setTimeout` after it unmounts. At the failing commit (d0dfada, PR #377) the spec pressed Escape and then `i` with no wait between them.
- `i` landed on the page body while the hand-back was pending. The invite dialog opened and focused Email address, so `toBeFocused` passed. Once typing began, the hand-back focused the button, outside the dialog. The dialog's focus trap pulled focus back to the field with `select: true`, and the next letter replaced every letter typed so far ("in" selected, "v" overwrote it).
- Why only sometimes: the hand-back has to fire after typing starts, which depends on how much work Chromium does between the keys. Usually it fires before the first letter.
- Evidence: an instrumented probe (since deleted) logged every focus call with its stack, every key and the field's value. With the failing commit's bare Escape then `i`, the hand-back fired after `i` in 9 of 30 runs. With Radix's hand-back timer delayed 150 ms, the old steps went red 8 of 10 on the exact symptom (e.g. `Received: "-1790557391757-570631@example.test"`), each losing exactly the letters typed before the hand-back. With the screen fix, the same bare-Escape steps were green 10 of 10.
- Fix: the screen fix is T-471's, landed in betteranswers/better-answers#475 (`keepFocusAKeyMoved` in `apps/web/src/shared/keystrokes.tsx`): the list keeps focus where a sooner key moved it, so the dialog's field is never robbed. This ticket's own commit adds one wait, in `sources.spec.ts` (~1082): `keystrokesDismissed` before `b` and `?`. Those keys could land in the still-closing list, which ignores them whatever the setting says, so that check could pass without testing anything. The invitation spec's own wait (`keystrokesDismissed`, 0e054be) had already landed on `main` after the failing run.
- `--repeat-each 30`: "an Admin resends and cancels an invitation by keyboard alone" passed 30 of 30 on Chromium 153.0.8010.12. Whole local suite: 192 passed, 10 failed; the 10 are the claude.ai-redirect tests in `consent.spec.ts` and `console-people.spec.ts`, which fail the same way on unchanged `main` in the cloud container. The merge group's `full-web` was green.
- Other specs read for the same hazard: `audit-log.spec.ts:262`, `people-requests.spec.ts:211` and `accept-invitation.spec.ts:259` send their next key after the list is gone, so #475's fix covers them and no wait was added. `sources.spec.ts:1075` was fixed here, as above. Already safe (wait for the button's focus): `console-people.spec.ts:418`, `sources.spec.ts:1061`, `people.spec.ts:293`, `people-groups.spec.ts:350`. Safe (no pending hand-back before typing): `ask-to-join`, `display-name`, `sign-in`, and `sources.spec.ts` 252/488/563. The `people.spec.ts` sheet closes (406–907) are followed by assertions, reloads or clicks, not typing.
