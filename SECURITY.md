# Security

Report a vulnerability to **security@better-answers.com**. Do not open a public issue for it.

You will get an acknowledgement within three working days and a fix or a decision within thirty. Reports are read by the maintainer only; nothing you send is shared without your say-so.

Scope: this repository and the hosted service at `better-answers.com`. Out of scope: the third-party services the platform runs on (GitHub, Cloudflare, IONOS, Backblaze) — report those to their owners.
