The app shell is shadcn Sidebar, not Kibo

Kibo has no shell. Build it from sidebar, breadcrumb, command (⌘K), separator and scroll-area, then dress it: 248px rail on --surface-sunken, 48px top bar, wordmark in Geist Mono, marks on the page region only. Kibo Blocks — Hero, Pricing, Testimonial — are for the marketing site, never the product.

Icon collision: Lucide vs Phosphor

Every kit ships Lucide. Leave it for internal furniture a reader never reads as an icon — chevrons, the ✕ on a dismiss. Swap to Phosphor via Icon for anything carrying product meaning. One decision, taken once, or the two sets drift together on screen.

Sources is mechanics; Citation is meaning

AI Elements Sources and Inline Citation do the fiddly part — anchoring a marker to a token mid-stream. Take that, discard the presentation. What renders is our Citation: concept, source, locator, passage, on one disclosure (ADR 0015).

Two words that mean the opposite

shadcn accent is a hover tint, not a brand colour, and shadcn primary is our near-black button, not ink blue. tokens/tailwind-bridge.css maps both correctly. Ink blue is exposed as brand, a name no kit overwrites.
