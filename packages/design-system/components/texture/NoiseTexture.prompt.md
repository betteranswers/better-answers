One-line: a 3.5–5% fractal-noise film over dark and accent surfaces — `@magicui/noise-texture` at the blueprint tokens.

```jsx
<div style={{position:"relative",background:"var(--accent-600)"}}>
  <NoiseTexture />
  <h1 style={{position:"relative"}}>…</h1>
</div>
```

Dark and accent surfaces only: the dark theme page, the dialog scrim, a full-bleed accent band, the app chrome. Never on a white card, never over reader prose, never above 5% — it is grain, and the moment it reads as a pattern it has failed. Suppressed automatically under `prefers-reduced-transparency`.
