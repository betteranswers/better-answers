One-line: the rare modal — for an irreversible act only; two levels of disclosure exist before it, and a modal is never the third ([UX1]).

```jsx
<Dialog open={open} title="Accept 12 concepts?" description="They enter the bundle as one commit."
  consequence="One governed write. Audited under your name."
  actions={<><Button variant="ghost" onClick={close}>Cancel</Button><Button variant="accent">Accept</Button></>}
  onClose={close}/>
```

Positioned `absolute` within the nearest positioned ancestor so it works inside a framed screen; Escape closes.
