# Design context for pixel-perfect

better-answers is a cited, permission-aware knowledge product for UK SMBs, used at work by Admins, Editors and Viewers, and held to WCAG 2.2 AA (`apps/web/AGENTS.md`).

Impeccable is its design authority. Before any design work, read the surface brief for the area in `apps/web/.impeccable/surfaces/`, and use the tokens in `apps/web/src/styles/`. The brief's Direction contract sets one direction for the area, so where a pixel-perfect skill's defaults differ from it, on palette, fonts, type sizes and weights, motion, corners, borders or components, the brief wins.
