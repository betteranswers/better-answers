## Goal

Editors and Viewers land on **Ask**, the first screen of a reader surface beside Control Centre, not on Control Centre's Questions screen. Control Centre stays the Admin's surface, and Questions keeps ADR 0017's words, *Answer audit* included. A short ADR records the reader surface and each role's home before the code lands.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] **ADR first.** A new ADR, with its `docs/adr/README.md` row, records:
  - a reader surface beside Control Centre and the console;
  - Editors' and Viewers' home is its Ask screen;
  - an Admin's home is unchanged;
  - Control Centre is still "the one Admin surface";
  - what stays open: an Editor's home once question sets land (S6), who may read the answer audit, and where an owning Editor decides.

  It cites ADR 0017's last clause (*Answers* on the door) and route S6's "a reader surface beside Control Centre".
- [x] **Glossary.** `CONTEXT.md` gains **reader surface**, and its **home** entry names Ask for Editors and Viewers.
- [x] **The surface.** The SPA's screen tables gain a third `Surface` beside `CONTROL_CENTRE` and `CONSOLE`, drawn by the same frame (icon rail, view list, top bar). It has one screen, **Ask** (`/ask`), with two unbuilt views: **New question** (the default) and **Your questions**.
- [x] **Homes.** An Editor and a Viewer who sign in, or who are sent home when lost, land on Ask. An Admin still lands on People.
- [x] **Links both ways.** The person menu on the reader surface links to Control Centre, and the person menu in Control Centre links to Ask. The operator's link to the console is unchanged.
- [x] **Unbuilt line.** Ask's unbuilt view reads, from the SPA's word table: "Ask in Claude for now. Your questions and their answers will be listed here." Questions loses its reader-home line and shows the ordinary unbuilt line.
- [x] **Questions untouched.** Its three views, their names and paths are unchanged.
- [x] **Specs.** The `home` and routes specs, and the unit tests that pinned `/questions/answer-audit` as a reader's landing, read the homes from the screen tables. A browser spec lands an Editor and a Viewer on Ask and follows both person-menu links.
- [x] The accessibility gate is green on Ask. `check` green, on the pull request and in the merge queue.
