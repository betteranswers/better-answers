## Assessment A: design review of the Knowledge area

### Design-specificity verdict

The concept page belongs to this product. The title block has an ink frame and registration marks. It sets the reference and dates in Geist Mono, shows the trust words as text on a tint, and leaves honest blanks such as "Not set" and "No owner". Nobody could drop it unchanged into Guru or Notion. The rest of the area looks like any admin kit. All knowledge, Collections and owners, Concept types, Exports and Knowledge base are each a title, a count line and a hairline table card. Only the grid backdrop and the square corners carry the Issued Drawing look on those pages. The guide has no drawing character at all. It is a stack of outline buttons.

### Emotional journey

- Peak: a verified concept. The framed block reads "Verified by Priya Shah · 3 March 2026" on green. It feels like a signed drawing.
- Valley: the guide. Line after line reads "isn't recorded yet" and "No Brief yet", beside identical buttons, with no pointer to what comes first. It reads as a failed audit. The out-of-date concept is a second valley: it shows amber and offers nothing to press.
- End: All knowledge ends on an alphabetical list, and Exports is neutral. Neither tells a curator that the work is done or what comes next.

### Strengths

1. The title block is the signature. At 1440 it sits level with the title, and at 390 it stacks cleanly. It uses only the closed trust words, and its blanks are honest.
2. Trust is never colour alone. The same word, icon and tint appear in Search, All knowledge, Knowledge base, the guides and the concept page.
3. The keyboard layer and the refusal copy are mature. Single keys can be turned off, ⌘K works, `aria-keyshortcuts` is set, and the editor's errors name the fix.

### Priority issues

- **[P1] The guide is a wall of equal buttons with no pointer.** (Guide.) PRODUCT.md principle 4 asks for a pointer to where to focus. A Bid Writer cannot tell what to do first, and at 390 the page is about 3,400px of buttons. Fix: open with one line that names the next step. Fold each section's missing facts into one "Add 3 missing facts" disclosure. Draw "Needs review" as a plain tag, and give each section one primary action. Command: `/impeccable distill`.
- **[P1] A concept cannot be re-checked from its own page.** (Concept page.) The brief's story names "request verification". The out-of-date and unverified concepts offer only Edit and Copy. "Review by: Not set" and "Owner: No owner" have no Set, and the deprecated concept does not link to its replacement. Fix: put Verify (for an Admin or the owner) or "Ask the owner to check" in the trust cell. Add Set beside Review by and Owner, and a "Replaced by <link>" line on a deprecated concept. Command: `/impeccable harden`.
- **[P1] The title block comes last in reading order.** (Concept page.) `TitleBlock` renders after the body, Sources and Links in the DOM (`apps/web/src/features/knowledge/concept-page.tsx:417`), and CSS `order-3` lifts it on screen. A screen reader reaches trust last, against principle 1. At 390, Tab jumps backwards. Fix: render the block after the head in the DOM, and place it with grid columns, not `order`. Command: `/impeccable audit`.
- **[P2] The editor sits on top of a live read view.** (Concept editor.) The body, Sources and Links repeat below Save and Close, so the page doubles in length. Edit and the tags stay above the form. Footnotes show as `source-1` chips, not [1]. The editor's h3 follows the page h1, which the detector also flags. Fix: hide the read body, Sources and Links while editing. Show Edit as pressed, draw the chips as [1] and [2], and make "Edit concept" an h2. Command: `/impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] All knowledge is called a worklist but does not act like one.** (All knowledge, Collections and owners.) It sorts A to Z, has no Review by column, and has no "needs attention" order. Collections shows "50%" verified with no route to the unverified concepts. Fix: sort out of date first, then unverified, then ownerless. Add Review by, and link each Verified percentage to the matching filter. Command: `/impeccable clarify`.

### Persona red flags

- **Bid Writer:** in search the green trust tag is louder than the 14px title. Copy does not say what format it copies. There is no word or character count, though portals set limits per question. "Used in 2 answers" is not a link. On the guide every section reads "No Brief yet", so there is nothing to copy.
- **Knowledge Curator:** a concept has no Verify action. All knowledge is not ordered by need. Nothing explains "Types with 2 or fewer concepts". Bulk actions stay hidden until a row is selected.
- **Founder-Operator:** "4 of 21 facts included" and "50%" are counts, not pointers. `okf-extract/0.4`, the path reference and "Generated by: Not recorded" read as machine output.
- **Occasional Expert:** "cyber essentials plus" finds nothing, though the Corporate guide has a slot of that name. On most concepts "Owner: No owner" leaves nobody to ask. On a verified concept the answer comes well inside a minute.
- **Sam (accessibility):** trust is last in reading order. Tags look like buttons but are list items. At 390, Collections clips its Verified column with no sign that it scrolls. The sidebar's "Browse" and "Curation" are h3s before the page's h1.
- **Casey (mobile):** the title block (about 450px) comes before the body, so the answer starts below the first screen. Edit and Copy sit at the top. Tags and toolbar buttons are about 32px high, under 44px. The guide is about 3,400px long.

- The Status cell repeats the trust word, so "Out of date" and "Deprecated" each appear twice in one block.
- An empty half-cell sits beside Sources on the verified concept.
- The reference path wraps mid-word, as `safeguarding-children-` / `partnerships`.
- The body's "See also" line repeats the Links section.
- Download buttons use the export (upward) icon.
- The shortcut list mixes "Control+Enter" with "⌘K".
- Some page heads have a count line about 58px under the title. Search and Exports have none, and no page has a description.
- Knowledge base opens on "Insurance Policy in Corporate" without saying why.
- "8 sections need review" on the guide list looks like a button.
- The breadcrumb names the menu groups "Browse" and "Curation".
- At 390, two Search descriptions leave one word on their second line.

### Questions

1. Should the title block be where a person acts, with Verify, Set owner and Set review date in its cells?
2. Should the guide open on one sentence and one button, the next thing to do, instead of 21 slots?
3. Is All knowledge just Search sorted by need? Does the area need both?
