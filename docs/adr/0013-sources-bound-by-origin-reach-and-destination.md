# A source is bound by origin, reach and destination; a connected source is indexed on every run and, once its plan is accepted, extracted on every run; a referenced source is a read tool; a binding is published by an Admin act separate from sensitivity and audience; cited evidence outlives its source

A **source type** is two axes: **origin** — whose knowledge the source carries: the *company*'s own, a third party's (*external*) or the *platform*'s — and **reach** — whether the platform holds a copy: a **connected** source is bound, enumerated and indexed by runs; a **referenced** source is bound with a credential and read live, never enumerated or indexed. Platform origin is an evidence kind with no binding (a record the platform generated, cited by a promoted concept); the bundle is never a source — derive-and-sync over the workspace repository is a `graph_sync_run` job on the shared scheduler, not a `source_binding`. A **source binding** is an Admin's connection of one source to the workspace: connector, credential (class per `[SEC1]`, injected per run), typed include/exclude scope with exclusion winning, one `domain_path` (ADR 0003 — a website is bound per URL prefix), `sensitivity`, `audience`, cadence, **destination** (ADR 0011 amendment: chunk index · bundle · graph, at least one; the object store is the landing stage), `provider` and **retention class**. The **v0.1 roster** is five connectors an Admin can bind: **upload** (once, through the object store — the first client's four bid libraries, one per domain), **website** (per URL prefix, sitemap mode, allowed-host policy), **SharePoint** (required by the first client), **referenced** (credential plus an HTTP read tool over an allowed-page list — the ISMS, Companies House) and **file_share** (a folder on a client's own network, served by the share agent: `credential_kind: agent_token`, `cadence: push`, ADR 0005/0008 amendments). HubSpot and Asana are lifted and dark; there is no feed connector (intelligence feeds are out of v0.1), no OKF-bundle binding (an existing bundle enters through the governed batch write, ADR 0012) and no worker-local folder watch — a "local folder" in v0.1 is a folder inside a connected system, or the on-prem share through the agent.

**What a run does.** Every run of a connected binding indexes: it lands originals blob-before-row, reconciles the **source document** catalogue (source-system id as identity, locator, `last_modified` recorded absent when the source has none, content hash, first/last seen, `gone_at`), converts through the worker's own conversion step (`RawFileCallback`, a converter per provider, the redaction seam, per-document isolation with a `quarantined` outcome) and embeds incrementally. Extraction is priced at the **review** step from a slim enumeration and accepted once as the binding's **extraction plan** — documents × template per kind × route, hours and pounds from measured per-template, per-route rates; from then on a targeted binding indexes and extracts together on every run, within the workspace's extraction ceiling, and a run that would reprocess more than N % of the binding waits for re-acceptance with the price shown. Extraction runs whenever the destination includes `bundle` or `graph`; until a plan is accepted the binding shows its unextracted backlog. A **connector run** is the scheduler's unit — claimed with `SKIP LOCKED` under a lease renewed by heartbeat, keyed by its run key, checkpointed per batch into the object store, one run per binding at a time, parked after a poison threshold of failed attempts; its outcome rows record changes only.

**Reaching a referenced source.** A producer enriching concepts from an Admin-named referenced source reads it live through the binding's credential; the tool's return passes the redaction seam and carries the binding's sensitivity; nothing is cached, chunked or indexed. The citation is the concept file's own `sources[]` entry — `resource`, `id`, `title`, `author`, `last_modified` (spec) and `locator` (ours) — and the derived `evidence` row that indexes it; *changed since checked* for a referenced resource is derived by re-fetching and comparing against the `last_modified` and hash the row recorded. There is no evidence-snapshot record: the concept backed by a resource with its own trust signals *is* the value (Liam, 27/08/2026).

**Publish.** A binding is **published** by one recorded Admin act, separate from sensitivity, from audience and from accepting suggestions; until then its chunks and source entities reach Admins only, in Control Centre's preview. The act's audit row carries the Admin's confirmations — lawful basis recorded, privacy information updated, DPIA reference (research 51). Permission-awareness in v0.1 is three binding fields — `published_at`, `sensitivity`, `audience` (`everyone` or group ids) — plus the carried per-document `external_access` shape (`is_public = true`, a no-op term), applied as one server-side predicate on every chunk read, every graph read and every traversal hop; the `audience` column ships in the first migration, the group UI does not, and no source-system ACL is synchronised.

**Retention.** Three classes: **mirror** — the source holds the record; a document gone at source keeps its chunks through a 28-day grace period and is flagged, then loses them; disconnecting the binding removes them within a day; **keep** — the platform holds the record (uploads); nothing leaves without an Admin act; **transient** — original bytes deleted after processing, the normalised redacted text kept (graph-only bindings). In every class **cited evidence outlives its source**: the evidence row and the cited span's hash stay until nothing cites them; Control Centre lists the impact of a deletion (concept · span hash matches last seen · citations on the concept); an uncited gone document appears nowhere; an agent pass analyses the impact of deletions and separate runs propose repairs for citations of deprecated or gone sources.

We decided this because the first client's knowledge arrives from four shapes at once — files handed over, a SharePoint site, a public website and systems it will cite but never connect — and one binding shape with two axes covers all of them without a type per case; because indexing is cheap and reversible while extraction spends money and lands in a permanent history, so the two are gated differently (index automatically; price and accept once, then keep up); because a live read through a credential is the only honest way to cite a system the company has chosen not to copy; because publishing is a legal act with confirmations no vendor records, and sensitivity alone cannot express "not yet" or "sales only"; and because a concept's citation must survive the document it rests on or trust is a lie.

## Considered options

- **Three axes** (origin · index · write, ticket 15's briefing) or the draft's **flat five** source types — "write" is constant (only the platform writes) and "evidence-only" has no v0.1 case; the pair is enough.
- **Extract only under a plan each time**, or **index only with a standing plan off by default** (17 rounds 1–2) — prototype 52 showed the switch *is* the plan: for a targeted binding, extracting on every run costs one document's re-extraction per edit and keeps the bundle current.
- **Sensitivity as the only gate**, or **no gate** — cannot say "indexed, not yet released" or "sales only"; retrofitting an ACL term later is Onyx's whole EE layer (research 51).
- **URL-only citations**, **caching the referenced page**, or an **evidence snapshot** record (span, hash, `fetched_at`) — withdrawn on Liam's layer argument: the concept file's `sources[]` is the citation; a stored copy is a copy the company chose not to make.
- **Mirror only**, or **several numeric retention classes** — uploads have no source to mirror; graph-only bindings need transient; more classes than three had no case.
- **A customer-hosted worker now** for the on-prem folder (v1.0), a **worker-local folder watch**, or a **platform drop area** — the share agent reaches the folder the platform cannot, with nothing of the platform on the client's LAN.
- **Everything lifted on the menu** (HubSpot and Asana lit), an **OKF-bundle upload binding**, **source-ACL synchronisation** — no first-client case; each stays dark or behind an existing act.

## Consequences

- Control Centre's Sources section holds two gates — **publish** (chunks and source entities) and **accept** (concepts, ADR 0012) — plus review, plans, the unextracted backlog and the extraction ceiling (ticket 37).
- Record families: `source_binding`, `connector_run` with outcome rows, `source_document`, `extraction_plan`, `credential` (classes per `[SEC1]`, including the agent token), the publish act's audit row; no `evidence_snapshot` (ticket 16).
- Named signals: unextracted backlog, indexed-but-unpublished bindings, parked runs, quarantined documents, credential expiry, grace expiries, gone-at-source evidence (ticket 42).
- Search, answers and MCP never return chunks from an unpublished binding or from a referenced source; every hit carries its binding's sensitivity and audience (tickets 20, 21).
- The redaction seam sits on two paths — conversion and the read tool's return; a `Person` from a referenced fetch starts restricted (ticket 24). *Gone at source* is a trust state (ticket 23).
- The Onyx lift is the narrow one ticket 12 decided — the utility layer plus `file`, `web`, `sharepoint`, `hubspot`, `asana`; `file`, `hubspot` and `asana` are not Slim; `LIFT.md` records the cut of `extract_text_and_images`, the pinned defaults and their code-vs-docs discrepancies; one deliberate snapshot refresh a quarter with a contract test against a real SharePoint site. The website connector runs in sitemap mode; recursive crawling with Playwright is ticket 41's (a separate image, or dropped from v0.1).
- Jobs beside runs: the 28-day grace sweep, prune, orphan-blob GC; object-store prefixes per workspace (ticket 41).
- A DPIA precedes the first client's ingest (research 51); every scale number depends on the estate size, so a slim enumeration of the real SharePoint site precedes any priced plan.
- `CONTEXT.md` gains *source binding, connector, origin, connected source / referenced source, source document, connector run, extraction plan, publish (a binding), audience, retention class*; "ingest trace" retires.
- Reopens nothing in ADRs 0001–0012; stands on the ticket-53 amendments to 0005, 0007, 0008 and 0011; ADR 0003's one-domain-per-binding rule holds.

## Amendment — 2026-08-27, the seam's output, findings and the widening block (ticket 24, ADR 0020)

The redaction seam's output is versioned by rule version and detector pin per document; a re-detection re-baselines evidence as a platform act and never raises *source moved on*. Findings (category, offsets into the normalised text) are shown on the enumeration sample, at the extraction-plan review grouped by category × rule, and in the publish dialog whose audit row carries the totals and the DPIA input's hash. There is no publish block; **widening** a binding or document is blocked while it holds unreviewed special-category findings. The route record carries a `retention_tail` per route the DPIA input and the erasure report state.

## Amendment — 2026-08-29, templates and kinds (ticket 50, ADR 0026)

An extraction template names whatever kinds the document kind evidences; there is no design-time validator against a vocabulary because there is no vocabulary file. The concept / source-entity split stands; a source-entity type that shares a name with a concept kind uses the same word; kinds fold case and plural at write. The platform bundle's extraction guidance carries the starter kinds ("prefer these — a better short kind is fine"). A suggestion set's summary names any kind it introduces.

## Amendment — 2026-08-30, the lift label (ticket 79, the pre-build gate; applied by T-001)

Where the consequence above says "`LIFT.md` records the cut of `extract_text_and_images`, the pinned defaults and their code-vs-docs discrepancies", the file at the root of each `<tier>/lifts/<name>/` snapshot is **`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`** — same contents, the name other engineers recognise (ADR 0027, `[LIFT1]`).
