# The graph is Neo4j Community on one instance, tenanted by rule, written in generations and never a hard dependency

The platform's derived graph (ADR 0011) is **Neo4j Community on the 5.26 LTS line** (`5.26.30-community` on the day; supported to 06/06/2028; the next LTS is a rebuild, not a migration), one container in VPC 1 on the private network, never a public IP, no plugins, run unmodified over Bolt so its GPLv3 touches nothing the platform links. All fifty workspaces share the instance: **every node and relationship carries `workspace_id`**; the uniqueness constraint on every label is composite — `(workspace_id, gen, uid)` on the bundle-and-record partition, `(workspace_id, uid)` on the source-entity partition — so `Actor`, `Tag` and `Source` are never shared across workspaces; Community has no existence constraints, so presence is the writer's guarantee (`[DESIGN4]`, tested) plus a nightly audit. **No graph-side vector or full-text index is a retrieval route**: hits come from Postgres (ADR 0016), the graph is entered by IRI or entity id through the constraint-backed lookup and expanded outward, depth ≤ 4. Every statement is a prepared template from one graph query module per tier with `workspace` from the session principal and a per-query timeout; instance-per-tenant (`workspace.graph_instance`) is the isolation step, a compose change; Enterprise is the step after that, not planned.

**The sync is atomic by generation.** The bundle-and-record partition — our own derive over the Python driver — carries `gen` on every node and edge. An ordinary `graph_sync_run` (a scheduler job kind woken by `bundle_commit` and by a record watermark, verify commits skipped, one run per workspace under a per-workspace lock, a 5-second debounce capped at 30 seconds) reads the workspace's live generation at claim and rewrites **each changed concept's subgraph in one transaction** by scoped key — a crash leaves the graph *behind*, never worse, and the resume is idempotent. A **full rebuild** (`reason`: first-sync · route-change · reconciler · erasure · upgrade · drill) writes the next generation beside the live one, cross-checks the parse against the app-written concept index (a mismatch stamps anyway and raises `bundle_health` as a state), and the swap is **one row update, `workspace.graph_live_gen`**, which every read binds; the retired generation is swept per label in batches after a 60-second grace — **for an erasure the flip and the sweep are one step**, and the sweep's last commit is the timestamp on the `erasure_request` report. The **source-entity partition** is written and reconciled per document by cocoindex's Neo4j target (`managed_by="user"`), carries no generation, passes the read predicate on `binding_id`, and is erased by suppression + reprocess (ADR 0020), never by a flip.

**Keys are never text** (prototype 52): concepts by IRI, sections by `(IRI, slug)`, source entities by `(document, type, normalised-text hash)`, actors by `(workspace, actor id)`, compositions by `(record id, version)`. A confirmed alias merge is an **`entity_merge` rule** — an audit event, never a commit — producing a **canonical entity**: a node of its own keyed by the rule, carrying no `binding_id`, with *every* contribution hanging off it by `SAME_AS` under its own binding, class and audience; a canonical with no contribution visible to the caller is never returned; undo is deleting the rule and re-deriving; a `Person` concept links by `IS_CONCEPT` and is never merged into.

**The read predicate lives once**, in the app's graph query module, **inline in quantified path patterns** on every node and relationship (the only form Neo4j 5.26 prunes during expansion): `workspace_id = $workspace` and then, by partition, `gen = $gen` (plus the concept's own sensitivity where the node is a concept) or `published_at` ∧ sensitivity ∧ audience for the caller. Templates are built through a builder that cannot emit a pattern without it. Three functional tests ship with the module: a Person merged from a published and an unpublished binding returns only the published contributions to a non-Admin; a query without `$workspace` throws in every environment; a Restricted `Person` concept reached through `IS_CONCEPT` by a Viewer outside its names returns nothing and hints nothing.

**When the map is down or behind, nothing that the rows can stand behind stops.** Search, badges, guides and cited answers work from Postgres; `ask` still finds entry points, judges and reuses an `Answer` at depth 0 and its verdict is computed as usual — the degradation is the answer's context header line, in the reader's word **map**: *map as of <time>* · *map as of <time> · updating* · *map unavailable since <time>*; impact, successors and coverage show the phrase and what still works; the successor chase falls back one hop to the successor IRI the concept index row carries; MCP carries a `map` field with the three values, never a count; a map-drawn read has a per-request budget after which it degrades.

**Not backed up; rebuilt inside a budget shown as headroom.** Community's only consistent backup is `neo4j-admin database dump` on a stopped database; the graph is rebuilt from git, records and the normalised text *transient* keeps. A nightly test on a deterministic fifty-workspace fixture asserts the estate rebuild under 60 minutes and shows it as headroom with a signal at 75 %; the per-workspace figure (≤ 2 minutes) is the promise on the erasure report; the monthly drill rebuilds one workspace on the restore target, diffs node and edge counts per label against production, and exercises the sweeper. **`neo4j-graphrag` is not a runtime dependency**: its resolvers merge nodes in place with `apoc.refactor.mergeNodes` across the database, so only its three matchers (exact · RapidFuzz · spaCy cosine) are lifted under `apps/worker/lifts/` as a proposal generator writing pending `entity_merge` rows from the redacted text, per binding; no retriever is a route; no APOC.

## Considered options

- **Apache AGE inside the one Postgres** — one stateful service fewer, but no Bolt (cocoindex's Neo4j target and every Cypher asset rewritten as `cypher()`-in-SQL over `agtype`, a Node driver not on npm), a schema per tenant under the app's migrations, and a cadence behind Postgres majors. The named alternative if instance-per-tenant is ever needed for more than a few workspaces.
- **An edges table with recursive CTEs** — full tenancy for free at the price of every traversal with a predicate on every hop becoming SQL. The fallback if the graph ever has to leave the estate.
- **An instance per tenant from day one** — VPC 1's memory ten times over for a store a Postgres predicate already guards.
- **Clear + rebuild in one transaction per workspace** — meets `db.memory.transaction.max` at the fiftieth workspace and holds write locks for the run's length. **Deltas only** — a route change, an erasure re-derive and the reconciler need a safe "rewrite everything".
- **The canonical entity as the target contribution's node** — wears one binding's predicate and gates the whole person; fails the merged-Person test whichever side an Admin picks. **Rewriting merged entities into one node** — loses the per-contribution predicate and cannot be undone.
- **`ask` refuses while the map is down** — refuses answers the rows can stand behind, for a store ADR 0004 says is never a hard dependency.
- **A nightly offline dump** — a minute of *map unavailable* to restore what a rebuild reproduces exactly; a dump older than the last commit invites a restore instead of a rebuild.
- **`neo4j-graphrag`'s resolvers as the proposal generators** — wrong on the facts (research 67); **keeping the package for its `Component`/`Pipeline` shapes** — two hundred lines of shape for a package whose every behaviour is forbidden.

## Consequences

- **Records**: `graph_sync_run` (workspace, bundle commit SHA, record watermark, gen, mode, reason, started, finished, outcome, counts); `entity_merge` (pending · confirmed; in ADR 0014's erasure union — it keys contributions by a hash of a name); `workspace.graph_live_gen` and `workspace.graph_instance`; the concept index row carries the resolved successor IRI at commit time.
- **DDL the app owns** (ADR 0007): the composite uniqueness constraints per label — Concept, Section, Source, Actor, Composition, Evidence, CanonicalEntity and the source-entity types — and a range index on `gen` per label for the sweeper; `db.memory.transaction.max`, `db.memory.transaction.total.max`, `db.transaction.timeout` and the heap and page-cache sizes from `neo4j-admin server memory-recommendation --docker` are part of the deploy unit; `NEO4J_AUTH` is a bootstrap secret; the compose file sets no `db.format`.
- **Signals** (ticket 42): reachable; map age per workspace, worst first; last run as a state (ok · mismatch at <IRI> since · failed ×N since); rebuild (none · in progress · last took t of 2 m); estate headroom (t of 60 m, nightly); the nightly presence audit. The generation number stays off the screen.
- **`CONTEXT.md`**: *map* (the reader's word for the graph; *graph*, *sync*, *traversal* and *generation* never reach a surface) with its three phrases; *generation*; *graph sync run*; *entity merge*; *canonical entity*.
- **`[DESIGN4]`** gains its graph clause (composite keys; presence by test and audit; `$workspace` and `$gen` bound from the session and the workspace row; the inline predicate on every hop through the builder; no graph-side index as a route; no APOC; no `neo4j_graphrag` import).
- **The first build task's spike** settles what the material could not: Neo4j transaction-log retention and store-file reuse after an erasure; depth-4 cost with and without a relationship range index on `gen`; whether the composite index alone serves the sweeper; transaction memory against the largest `Answer` subgraph; that cocoindex's target writes `workspace_id`, `binding_id` and the class on every node and edge and deletes a suppressed document's entities on reprocess; the matchers' thresholds for a human-confirmed queue; the builder's shape; the fixture; Bolt hardening on the private network.
- Amends ADRs 0004, 0005, 0007, 0014, 0016 and 0020 as recorded on each. Research 55's open question 6 (Community backup without stopping) is answered: it cannot; it need not.
