# Records attach to concepts by IRI and never restate them; three families keep versions; one ledger records every act; the app writes every bundle-derived row at commit time; a context wording is a section of its concept

Every record the platform keeps is one of four kinds, sorted by the minting rule (ADR 0011): **derived from the bundle** — the concept index, `concept_identity`, `bundle`, `bundle_commit` (with a `bundle_commit_concept` row per concept changed, before and after hash) and `evidence`, all written by the app in the commit's transaction, rebuilt from git, never edited; **attached to a concept by IRI** — `verification`, `verification_request`, `concept_owner`, `conflict`, `usage`, `suggestion`, `export`; **use-case records** — `guide_definition` and its `section` rows, `composition` with two homes (a guide section, a response) and its `composition_include` rows, `question_set` and its `question` rows, the `answer_audit` with `feedback` and `correction`; and **platform state** — the source families ADR 0013 fixed plus `suppression` and `erasure_request`, identity (`workspace`, Better Auth's `user` · `member` · `team`, `api_token`), `route`, `llm_call`, `worker_instance`, `graph_sync_run`, `backup_run`, `platform_event` and one `audit_event` ledger. Six rules bind them. **(1) Attach, never restate.** Every record about a concept references the concept index row by `(workspace_id, iri)` — a composite foreign key — and carries nothing the file states; an organisation the company knows is a concept, a contact is a later record attached to it, a certificate is a `Certification` concept with `stale_after` as its expiry. **(2) Rows, never saved lists.** A composition's includes, a definition's sections and a set's questions are rows with platform-minted stable ids, so a changed or discarded concept flags every citing composition by index lookup, "used by N" is a `GROUP BY`, and nothing is orphaned silently; each include records the concept's content hash at generation. **(3) Versions only where a person edits text.** `composition` and `guide_definition` keep append-only `_version` tables sharing one column set (version number, actor, at, note) with typed content; `needs_review` and its reason live on the head row; every other family is current state whose changes are audit events; concept history is git. **(4) One act, one event, one typed row.** `audit_event` — one row per act *and* target with a batch id, insert-only, partitioned by month with retention per kind, exportable — records every Admin and platform act (the publish act's LIA · privacy-notice · DPIA confirmations are its typed detail; `bundle_commit` carries its event's id), written in the same transaction as the typed row it describes; the `answer_audit` (content, under retention) is a separate table. **(5) Derived rows have one writer.** The app writes the concept index, `bundle_commit_concept` and `evidence` at commit time — from the suggestion's payload when the worker proposed the concept, from the file's `sources[]` when a person wrote it; `evidence` keeps its identity across commits (natural key, `retired_at`, never deleted, because conflicts point at it); the worker's re-fetch observations are append-only `evidence_check` rows; a concept citing another concept is a graph edge, never an evidence row; the worker's sync cross-checks. **(6) A context wording is knowledge, not a record.** An alternative wording of a concept's statement demanded by a context (a buying framework, a regulation, a sector) is a named section of the concept's body, chosen by an include — the client keeps 89 such wordings today with no platform — so it carries the concept's history, portability and `verified` state; the *phrasing variant* family and its version table do not exist. `content_hash` is body plus frontmatter minus the trust keys (`verified`, `generated`), so verifying a concept never makes it *changed since checked*; the file's actor ids are opaque platform ids, never an email; `usage` records only acts that leave the platform (copied · exported-in-document · later submitted) — membership is a query over includes and citations are counted from the answer audit; owners are per domain with a per-concept override written at mint; erasure actions are an exhaustive typed union over every family that holds text; a `records export` kind takes verifications, owners, usage, conflicts, the audit ledger and the publish confirmations out with the company.

We decided this because the records are where the platform's promise — trust per fact, reviewed en masse, provenance visible before a person acts — is either kept or quietly lost: a saved list inside a column cannot be a foreign key, so a changed atom would reach its compositions by a scan or not at all; a version table with a mutable flag is not append-only; a hash that includes the check it records flips every verified concept the day it is checked; a usage table that grows with reads is the largest table in the estate within a year; a wording kept as a record wears the concept's badge without ever being checked; and every one of these is a line now and a migration with user-visible churn later. Two reviewers reached the same verdicts independently (research 56).

## Considered options

- **A parties family now** (organisation, relationship, contact — the draft's shape) — a second home for facts the bundle already states about a client, and personal data held for a use case that is not in v0.1.
- **Two composition families** (section, response) — the same columns twice and every cross-cutting query written twice; **a response as an audit row only** — no version to export or point back at.
- **No question-set record** (a batch key) — no title, no order, no re-run; **an upload binding as the question set** — a buyer's document through the pre-scan and publish gate as company knowledge.
- **Per-act audit tables** or **audit in the application log** — a union across tables, or nothing queryable by target.
- **One generic JSON version table** — no types, no typed diff, every family paying the version cost; **no versions, before/after in audit payloads** — history a UI cannot list, diff or restore.
- **Verification as columns on the row** — one event per target, a second reviewer overwriting the first, the never-edited index row edited.
- **Evidence derived by the worker after the commit** — a second writer of derived rows, a window with no evidence, flagging that cannot see it.
- **Usage recording every answer citation** — ~58 million rows a year at fifty workspaces, each naming a person, for a count the answer audit already gives.
- **The context wording as its own family** (ticket 46's move) or **as a third composition home** — a record a company loses on leaving, with no verification target; the minting rule, decided after 46's key ruling, governs units and places it in the file.
- **An evidence-artefact family** for certificates — a second expiry beside `stale_after`.

## Consequences

- ADR 0004's amendment "phrasing variants are platform records" is reversed under ADR 0011 (amended there); ticket 46's ruling on *keys* stands — no `variants:` key, no guide-shaped tag; a concept's `tags` may name the contexts it carries wordings or answers for, in company language (ticket 50 fixes the convention).
- ADR 0013's "publish act's audit row" is an `audit_event`; nothing in ADR 0013 changes.
- The opportunity layer attaches without re-keying: an opportunity references an Organisation concept by IRI; `pack_version_id` and `opportunity_id` are nullable references on `question_set` and on a response; a submission and an outcome attach to `usage` rows that name the composition version copied; the promotion gate mints `Answer` concepts through the governed write. Checked against the predecessor's intended schema (`old-suggested-schema.md`, Liam, 27/08/2026 — context only): its parties, agreements and lots, evidence artefacts and entity registers are concepts here; its `question` hierarchy, classes, obligations, dependencies and reference-question link are later columns on `question`; its response workflow (status, owner, deadline, reviewers, attachments) is later columns on a response composition; a reference question set is a later `question_set.kind`; its response versions, citations, verification events and Q&A versions are already `composition_version`, `composition_include`, `verification` and git. The next effort checks its records against this list before adding a family.
- The commit transaction writes the index, `bundle_commit_concept`, evidence and the flagging of citing compositions together; a bundle import is batched into commits of about a thousand files. Verify commits update the trust projection and skip the graph's stage-and-swap (ticket 39).
- An agent's review of a guide waits as a `suggestion` of kind *composition rewrite*, accepted per section by a person (tickets 19, 37); a `needs_review` reason *review finding* carries what is not a rewrite.
- The runtime role has insert-only rights on `audit_event` and every `_version` table; a leaver's user row is retired, never deleted. Range partitioning on `audit_event` and the composite `(workspace_id, iri)` keys are proved in a schema spike before the first migration (ticket 41).
- The question set's input object is restricted by default, pre-scanned, retained by rule and in the erasure map (ticket 24); the sensitivity pre-scan runs on it.
- `CONTEXT.md` gains *record family, verification, verification request, owner (of a concept), usage, conflict, question set, answer audit, audit event, erasure request, suppression, version (of a record), include, needs review, skeleton projection, context wording*; "phrasing variant" retires. Every table name in this ADR is implementation; the glossary carries the words.
- Reopens nothing in ADRs 0001–0003, 0005–0013; amends 0004.

## Amendment — 2026-08-27, the hash normaliser, the actor id and the actor alias (ticket 23, ADR 0019)

`verification.content_hash` is RFC 8785 canonical JSON of the frontmatter with `generated`, `verified`, `stale_after`, `status` and `iri` removed, scalars as source text, `sources[]` reduced to ordered `(resource, locator)` pairs with paths resolved to `/abs.md`, plus the body with `\r\n → \n`, trailing whitespace stripped and one trailing newline — replacing "body + frontmatter minus the trust keys". Every `verification` row carries `origin` (platform · imported) from commit; an imported event has no hash. A person's actor id in `generated.by` and `verified[].by` is **`human:<email>`** as in Google's samples (Liam, 27/08/2026), replacing "an opaque platform-minted actor id, never an email"; the erasure request (ticket 24) covers those strings. An **`actor_alias`** record (imported id → member, Admin-set) joins the attached-by-IRI kind. Everything else stands.

## Amendment — 2026-08-27, findings, subject requests, the member id (ticket 24, ADR 0020)

Two families join: `finding` (attached to `source_document`: category, offsets into the normalised text, rule version, detector pin; the *keep in text* act with its reason) and `subject_request` (access · erasure; the erasure map's per-store finder on the one-month clock). The member row carries a **member id** (ULID minted at member creation) beside the email; on a valid erasure request the member row is **pseudonymised** — the one exception to "retired, never deleted".

## Amendment — 2026-08-28, the entity-merge family and the record watermark (ticket 39, ADR 0021)

An **`entity_merge`** record (pending · confirmed) is the rule a confirmed alias-merge suggestion writes — an `audit_event`, never a bundle commit — and joins the exhaustive erasure union because it keys contributions by a hash of a name. The graph's record watermark is the `audit_event` id of the last composition, evidence, include, entity-merge or suppression change. Everything else stands.

## Amendment — 2026-08-29, aliases, kinds, the section hash and the Answer's domain (ticket 50, ADR 0026)

`concept_identity` derives its merge-key names from the concept's `title` and its *Also known as* body line — a confirmed concept alias is a kind-*edit* suggestion that adds the line, never an alias store; an Admin's *No* to "Are these the same thing?" is remembered per pair as an audit event. A kind rename or merge is one bulk commit that re-keys `concept_identity` in the same transaction. An include's section hash covers the section's subtree with the heading line excluded, normalised as the 2026-08-27 amendment normalises a body; a body with two headings that fold to the same text is refused at commit, an imported bundle's duplicates flagged. An `Answer` minted at the promotion gate lands in the domain of the concept it chiefly cites, else `company/answers/`, shown for override. No `vocabulary_term`, no plan record, no vocabulary suggestion kind exist. Everything else stands.
