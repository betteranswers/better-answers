# An audience is a word and a group-id array on every readable unit, combined by intersection with *everyone* the identity, an empty intersection forcing Restricted and never stored, and an implicit group a `group` row nothing here mints

**Where this came from.** The glossary's *audience* — everyone in the workspace, or named groups — was a text column that could only hold the first half, and the read predicate's third term had nothing to test (`docs/research/audience-representation-2026-09-01.md`). ADR 0023's amendment had settled how a *class* is derived — the most restrictive among the bindings of the evidence a concept cites — and left the audience's combining rule, the empty intersection and the named-people case open. This record is the T-006 spec's *Visibility derivation and audience* section (5 September 2026) as T-055 built it, reconciled with ADR 0038's people model, which owns the group table, the group ids and the origin discriminator and is cited here for each rather than restated.

**The representation is a word and an array, tied by one CHECK, on every readable unit.** `audience text NOT NULL` narrowed at the boundary to the closed pair *everyone · groups*, and `audience_groups text[]` beside it holding the platform-minted ids of the named groups (ADR 0038's ids, ADR 0035's minter), with one CHECK — `AUDIENCE_CHECK`, written once in `packages/schema/src/concept-tables.ts` and copied into the hand-written DDL under a test that reads the copies back — holding *everyone* to no array and *groups* to a non-empty array with no NULL element. The pair sits on `concept_index`, `composition`, `source_binding`, `concept_class_override`, `index.chunk`, `graph_node` and `graph_edge` — every unit the predicate is tested against (ADR 0023's 2026-09-05 amendment) and the binding it derives from. The predicate's third term is `audience = 'everyone' OR audience_groups && $groups`, the caller's group ids resolved on this call by the Principal resolver (ADR 0009: never carried on a credential), and it **fails closed at every edge**: an empty caller list overlaps nothing, a NULL array overlaps nothing, and a deleted group's dangling id is an id no caller holds (ADR 0038). The array carries no foreign key, because an array cannot, and the fail-closed overlap is the guarantee. The three terms are **conjoined**: an audience narrows a Restricted unit's Admins exactly as it narrows an Internal unit's members — fewer readers on every term, never more — and the Admin arm is the class's alone.

**Audiences combine by intersection, with *everyone* as the identity.** A unit resting on several — a concept on the bindings of the evidence it cites, a composition on its includes — is for the groups every one of them names; a unit for everyone contributes nothing to the intersection and narrows nobody. Sensitivity combines beside it as ADR 0023 says, by the most restrictive class; the two are one derivation (`derivedVisibility` in `packages/core/src/access`), applied in the order its riders bind: a recorded Admin override outranks everything, then the combination, then a unit resting on nothing takes its fallback — the writer's word on a creation, what the row holds on anything else — and the per-kind floor narrows the class and never widens it. **The derivation runs at write time as well as at narrowing**: the governed write derives the row it lands, so a concept is never readable for an instant at a class its evidence does not allow; a narrowing of a binding recomputes every concept citing its documents and then every composition including them, synchronously, in the narrowing act's own transaction; and the map's copies of the columns — the node and the edges that wear the from-concept's — are rewritten in that same transaction, so the walk never forks from the index. A citation whose document the catalogue does not hold contributes no binding: it cannot widen, because it derives nothing, and it cannot narrow, because there is nothing to narrow by.

**An empty intersection forces the unit Restricted, and is never stored.** Evidence from two disjoint audiences is a real state — the unit is for nobody — and it is represented as the most restrictive visibility there is, *Restricted* for *everyone*, which the conjoined predicate reads as Admins alone. It is never stored as *groups* over an empty list, because the CHECK refuses that shape and because an empty list is the natural zero value: a shape that means *nobody* today is the shape a bug writes tomorrow, and a bug must narrow rather than widen. Nor is it stored as *everyone* at the inherited class, which would union two audiences that share no member into the whole workspace.

**An implicit group is a `group` row, and nothing here mints one.** A Restricted binding's named-people audience — *members an Admin has named on the binding* — is represented as a group of ADR 0038's implicit origin whose id `audience_groups` names, exactly as an Admin-curated group's is: one grouping shape, one membership lookup per visibility check, and no second table an audience could name. Minting that group is the binding-management surface's act (B7), and this slice treats an audience as *everyone* wherever such a surface would be needed; the origin discriminator exists so that the surface, when it lands, has nowhere else to put it.

We decided this because the one representation that is at once fail-closed, a plain column on every readable unit, a one-line term in the one predicate and an UPDATE for the two-level cascade is the word beside the array (the research weighs the four candidates); because intersection is the only combination under which a reader who passes a derived unit passes every unit it rests on, which is what makes *derived* mean *no wider than the evidence*; because the empty intersection has to be representable without loosening the CHECK, and Restricted-for-everyone is the one value the predicate already reads as *nobody but Admins*; because a predicate in which Admins bypassed the audience would make the audience a term about Viewers and Editors only, and the glossary says it is about who may see; and because two grouping shapes are two cascades to keep honest, which is ADR 0038's argument and stands.

## Considered options

- **A single nullable array, NULL meaning everyone.** Rejected: the widest grant is the absent value, so a row a bug leaves unwritten is visible to the whole workspace, and the column surrenders its `NOT NULL`. The `'{}'`-means-everyone variant is worse for the same reason.
- **A join table per readable unit.** Rejected: the predicate stops being a column on the unit, *everyone* needs a sentinel row to fail closed, and the synchronous cascade becomes a DELETE and INSERT across five side tables inside the narrowing transaction.
- **Storing the empty intersection as *groups* over an empty list.** Rejected: the CHECK would have to admit the zero value, and the zero value would then mean *nobody* on purpose and *everyone was forgotten* by accident, with no way to tell the two apart.
- **Combining audiences by union.** Rejected: a concept minted from HR's document and Sales's would be for both, which is wider than either piece of evidence — the opposite of *most restrictive*.
- **An Admin arm on the audience term.** Rejected: an Admin outside a Restricted binding's named group would see what the binding withholds, and the audience would no longer be a statement about who may see.
- **A second table for a binding's named people.** Rejected by ADR 0038, and not reopened: the implicit origin on the one table is the whole difference.

## Consequences

- The pair lands by migrations 0019 (generated: the derivation's six tables, `concept_index.audience_groups`) and 0020 (hand-written: `index.chunk`, `graph_node`, `graph_edge`; FORCE on the six; the worker's role revoked), each CHECK read back against `AUDIENCE_CHECK` by the migration-ownership test and refused at the row by the RLS suite. The worker writes the pair on chunk rows as the binding holds it — the tier contract's visibility columns gain a fourth (ADR 0031) — and applies no rule of its own.
- The rules live once, in `packages/core/src/access`, beside the predicate's one renderer; the concepts slice runs the derivation at write time and on a narrowing (`recomputeVisibilitySourcedFrom`), the guides slice the second level (`recomputeCompositionsIncluding`), the sources slice the narrowing act (`sources.binding.narrowed`, refusing a widening and a group the workspace does not hold), and the override is `knowledge.concept.class_overridden`, an Admin's, creating the state `CONTEXT.md` calls *shared beyond its evidence*, which the evidence pane's read names.
- **Amends ADR 0023** in words, by this record: the audience's combining rule and the empty-intersection rule its amendment left open are settled here; its class rule, its two-level cascade and its floor stand. **Amends ADR 0013** in words: *published · sensitivity · audience* are the three permission fields still, and the audience is now two columns.
- `CONTEXT.md`'s *audience*, *group* and *shared beyond its evidence* entries stand unedited: the word means what it meant, and this record is how the columns carry it.
- Reopens nothing in ADRs 0001–0022, 0024–0037; cites ADR 0038 and is cited by it.
