title: "Three knowledge layers and the minting rule decide where every unit of knowledge lives"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a new kind of unit, a record family or a table that holds knowledge"
  - "Deciding whether something belongs in a concept file or in a platform record"
  - "Deriving map nodes or edges from sources, bundles or records"
---

The platform has three knowledge layers, sources, bundles and the map, and records are not a layer. A unit is a concept when a company with no platform would keep it as knowledge, and a record when it exists only because the platform runs a use case. Five sessions in a row put a platform need inside the concept file or a Q&A pair in the wrong store because no document said what the layers were.

## Rejected

- Records first: makes the customer's answer library unportable and gives one kind of knowledge two trust mechanisms.
- Bundle first, with guides and answers as narrator concepts: prose over atoms drifts by design, and the bundle fills with UI-shaped state.
