---
status: accepted
title: "One declared navigation list is the only source of which pages exist and who sees them"
applies_when:
  - "Adding an area, a menu group or a page, or deciding where a page belongs"
  - "Deciding who sees a page, by role or by owning a collection"
  - "Changing what an unbuilt or hidden address shows"
---

`apps/web/src/shared/navigation/areas.ts` declares every area, menu group and page with its address, whether it is `built`, and who sees it, by role or by owning a collection. The rail, the menu, the router, the breadcrumb and jump-to all read it. An address the person may not see, or whose page is not built, shows the same not-found page as one that never existed, so nobody learns what exists by guessing addresses or reading "Soon".
