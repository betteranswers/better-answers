---
status: accepted
title: "One declared navigation list is the only source of which pages exist and who sees them"
applies_when:
  - "Adding an area, a menu group or a page, or deciding where a page belongs"
  - "Deciding who sees a page, by role or by owning a collection"
  - "Changing what an unbuilt or hidden address shows"
---

One navigation list declares every area, menu group, and page. Each entry gives the page's address, whether the page is built, and who may see it. The rail, the menu, the router, the breadcrumb, and jump-to all read this list. No second source can disagree with it about which pages exist.

A person sees a page by role, or by owning a collection that the page serves. A Viewer who owns a specialism reaches that specialism's curation pages. Control Centre stays visible to Admins alone.

Some addresses show the same not-found page as an address that never existed: an address the person may not see, and an address whose page is not built. Nobody should learn what exists by guessing addresses.

## Rejected

- Marking unbuilt entries "Soon", or keeping them as destinations that say they are not built. Either one tells a person what exists.
- A page declared as a route beside the generated routes, outside the list. The visibility gate, the frame, and the breadcrumb would each need a second source.
