---
status: accepted
title: "One declared navigation list is the only source of which pages exist and who sees them"
applies_when:
  - "Adding an area, a menu group or a page, or deciding where a page belongs"
  - "Deciding who sees a page, by role or by owning a collection"
  - "Changing what an unbuilt or hidden address shows"
---

One navigation list declares every area, menu group and page, with its address, whether it is built and who may see it, and the rail, the menu, the router, the breadcrumb and jump-to all read it, so no second source can disagree about what exists. A page is seen by role or by owning a collection it serves, so a Viewer who owns a specialism reaches its curation while Control Centre stays the Admin's alone. An address a person may not see, or that is not built, shows the same not-found page as one that never existed, because nobody should learn what exists by guessing addresses.

## Rejected

- Unbuilt entries marked "Soon", or kept as destinations that say they are not built: nobody learns what exists by guessing addresses.
- A page as a route written beside the generated ones, outside the list: the visibility gate, the frame and the breadcrumb would each need a second source.
