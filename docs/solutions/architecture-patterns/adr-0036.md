title: "The worker composes cocoindex's blocks and writes only what the engine has no block for"
applies_when:
  - "Adding or changing a cocoindex pipeline, component, memo or target in apps/worker"
  - "Deleting a connected source's passage rows, for a wipe, an erasure or a rule change"
  - "Deciding whether the worker builds a piece of queue machinery or takes it from cocoindex"
---

The worker composes cocoindex's building blocks, per-component commit, memoisation, target sync and one `Environment` per connected source, and writes only what the engine has no block for, the queue, the catalogue, the landing and supervision. The one memo is the detector's and holds spans, never text. A sync over a standing store re-upserts nothing it believes it has landed, so any deletion of a connected source's passage rows is paired with its store's removal, or a withdrawn document's passages would stand.

## Rejected

- One memo over conversion and detection returning redacted text: every policy change re-detected, and an erasure had to remove the store.
- The `finding` table as the cache: a wipe deletes its unmarked rows, so a partial set reads as complete and a document goes silently un-redacted.
