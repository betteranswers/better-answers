One read predicate, `readableClause`, guards every node, edge, concept, write-up and passage, and a unit's sensitivity is derived most-restrictive over the connected sources of the evidence it cites, re-derived synchronously inside the narrowing action two levels down. A sensitivity is a record, not knowledge, because a company marks documents confidential, not each unit of what it knows, so it may be derived and recomputed. A queued recompute reopens the leak for the length of the queue.

## Rejected

- Deltas only, with no generations: a model choice change, an erasure re-derive and the reconciler need a safe "rewrite everything".
- Rewriting merged entities into one node: it loses the per-contribution predicate and cannot be undone.
