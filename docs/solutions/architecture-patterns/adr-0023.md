title: "One read predicate guards every map element, and a unit's sensitivity is derived most-restrictive"
applies_when:
  - "Writing a query that walks the map or reads a concept, a write-up or a passage"
  - "Adding a node, an edge or a column to the map tables"
  - "Changing how a concept's or a write-up's sensitivity and audience are derived"
  - "Adding a citation path to the governed write"
---

One read predicate, `readableClause` in `packages/core/src/access/index.ts`, guards every map node and edge, concept, write-up and passage by `published_at`, sensitivity and audience. A unit's sensitivity is the most restrictive among the connected sources of the evidence it cites, because a company marks documents and not each fact it knows. An action that narrows a source re-derives, in its own transaction, the concepts that cite it and the write-ups that include them, because a queued recompute would leave the leak open while the job waits.

## Consequences

- The reads that let an owner or an Admin work on a draft, and the audit log's concept names, apply `sensitivityAndAudienceClause` without `published_at`.
