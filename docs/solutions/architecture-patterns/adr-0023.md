title: "One read predicate guards every map element, and a unit's sensitivity is derived most-restrictive"
applies_when:
  - "Writing a query that walks the map or reads a concept, a write-up or a passage"
  - "Adding a node, an edge or a column to the map tables"
  - "Changing how a concept's or a write-up's sensitivity and audience are derived"
  - "Adding a citation path to the governed write"
---

One read predicate, `readableClause`, guards every node, edge, concept, write-up, and passage. A unit's sensitivity is the most restrictive sensitivity among the connected sources of the evidence it cites. The action that narrows a source re-derives that sensitivity synchronously, two levels down.

A company marks documents as confidential. It does not mark each unit of what it knows. A unit's sensitivity is therefore a record and not knowledge, and the platform may derive it and recompute it. The recompute is synchronous because a queued recompute would reopen the leak for as long as the job waits in the queue.

## Rejected

- Deltas only, with no generations. A change of model choice, a re-derive after an erasure, and the reconciler each need a safe way to rewrite everything.
- Rewriting merged entities into one node. That loses the predicate for each contribution, and it cannot be undone.
