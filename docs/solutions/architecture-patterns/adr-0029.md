title: "Business logic is capability slices over four store doors in packages/core, under one import-direction lint rule"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a slice, a store door or a directory under packages/core/src"
  - "Importing from one slice into another, or from a transport into packages/core"
  - "Writing a query against a table another slice owns"
  - "Adding a deployable under apps/"
---

`apps/` holds what deploys and `packages/` holds what other code imports. Business logic lives in `packages/core` as capability slices over four store doors, `postgres`, `git`, `map` and `objects`, plus a kernel of `Principal`, the branded ids and `Result`. The `import-direction` lint rule places both ends of every import in a zone and checks the direction between them. The logic sits outside `apps/api` because four of its five callers have no notion of an HTTP status.

## Rejected

- Vertical slices that never import from each other. The slices share one database, so the rule forces duplicated queries or a `shared/` directory.
