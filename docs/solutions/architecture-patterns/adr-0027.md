status: accepted
title: "Better Answers is open core under Apache-2.0, and the hosted service is the product"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a dependency or lifting third-party code"
  - "Proposing a paid-only feature or an enterprise-licensed directory"
---

The whole repository is public under Apache-2.0, with no `ee/` directory and no paid-only feature. A customer pays for the hosted service. The product's claim is an open format the customer owns, and that claim holds only if the customer can leave with the bundle and run the software that reads it.

A dependency or lifted code must be under MIT, BSD, ISC, Apache-2.0 or the PostgreSQL licence. Copyleft software runs unmodified as a separate process that the platform reaches over the network, and is never linked or copied into the tree.

## Rejected

- A source-available licence, such as BSL or FSL. The product could no longer call itself open, and every customer's legal team would review a bespoke licence.
- AGPL-3.0. An IT manager would refuse to install the share agent.
