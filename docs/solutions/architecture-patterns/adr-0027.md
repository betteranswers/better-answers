The repository is public under Apache-2.0, one licence over the whole tree with no `ee/` directory and no paid gate, and what a customer pays for is the hosted service, while a dependency or lifted code is MIT, BSD, ISC, Apache-2.0 or PostgreSQL only and copyleft software runs unmodified as a separate process over a network protocol, never linked or copied. The product's one claim no competitor makes, an open format you own, is whole only when the customer can leave with the bundle and run the thing that reads it, and the share agent is sold to people who will read the source first. Apache-2.0 over MIT for the patent grant and the reservation of the name, which has no registered trade mark.

## Rejected

- Source-available, a Business or Functional Source Licence: it costs the word _open_ and hands every customer's legal team a bespoke licence.
- AGPL-3.0: an IT manager refuses the share agent, to guard against a competitor that does not exist yet.
- An `ee/` directory for paid features: it doubles every seam, and there is no feature to gate that is not also what makes the product usable.
