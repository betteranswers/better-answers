title: "A workspace keeps one bundle of atom concepts, organised collection-first, in v0.1"
applies_when:
  - "Deciding whether a piece of knowledge is one concept, two, or a section of its parent"
  - "Writing extraction or enrichment that proposes concepts"
  - "Proposing a second bundle per workspace, or a bundle per collection"
---

A workspace's knowledge is one OKF bundle laid out by collection, `knowledge/<collection>/<slug>.md`, and each concept is an atom: one entity or fact that can be verified, go stale or change owner on its own. The platform sells trust per fact, which a page of several facts hides, and splitting pages later would re-key every link and every verification. The collection-first layout makes a later move to one bundle per collection a promotion, not a rewrite.

## Rejected

- One bundle per collection now. OKF has no cross-bundle link, so every reference between collections would need a platform extension.
