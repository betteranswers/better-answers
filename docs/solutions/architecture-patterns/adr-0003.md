title: "A workspace keeps one bundle of atom concepts, organised collection-first, in v0.1"
applies_when:
  - "Deciding whether a piece of knowledge is one concept, two, or a section of its parent"
  - "Writing extraction or enrichment that proposes concepts"
  - "Proposing a second bundle per workspace, or a bundle per collection"
---

A workspace's knowledge is one OKF bundle. Each concept in it is an atom: one entity or one fact that can be verified, go stale, or change owner on its own. The bundle is organised collection-first, so moving later to one bundle per collection is a promotion and not a rewrite.

The platform's value is trust per fact. A page that holds several facts hides the `verified` and `stale_after` of each one. The unit was fixed before anything was built, because splitting pages into atoms later would re-key every link and every `verified` event.

## Rejected

- One concept per guide subsection, about 100 files. A page hides per-fact trust. A guide also cannot be the unit, because every company defines its own guides.
- One bundle per collection. OKF has no cross-bundle link, so every reference between collections would need a platform extension.
