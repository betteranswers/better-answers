title: "A workspace keeps one bundle of atom concepts, organised collection-first, in v0.1"
applies_when:
  - "Deciding whether a piece of knowledge is one concept, two, or a section of its parent"
  - "Writing extraction or enrichment that proposes concepts"
  - "Proposing a second bundle per workspace, or a bundle per collection"
---

A workspace's knowledge is one OKF bundle whose concepts are trust-bearing atoms, each an entity or fact that can be verified, go stale or change owner on its own, organised collection-first so a bundle per collection stays a promotion rather than a rewrite. The platform's value is trust per fact, and a page would hide the `verified` and `stale_after` of each fact it contains. Splitting pages into atoms later would re-key every link and every `verified` event, which is why this was decided before anything was built.

## Rejected

- A concept per guide subsection, about 100 files: a page hides per-fact trust, and a guide cannot be the unit when every company defines its own.
- A bundle per collection: OKF has no cross-bundle link, so every cross-collection reference would need a platform extension.
