status: accepted
title: "A connected source is defined by origin, reach and destination, gated by three permission fields"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a connector, a connected source field or a media type's converter"
  - "Writing a read of passages, source entities or concepts that must apply the permission predicate"
  - "Changing how a connected source's or a document's sensitivity is narrowed, widened or published"
---

A connected source is described by its origin (the company or an external party), its reach (copied, or read live and never stored) and its destinations (`passage-index`, `bundle`, `map`), so a new connector is a point on two axes and not a new source type. The `upload` connector, company and copied, is the only one built, and each media type it admits has one converter. Three permission fields gate what a source releases, `published_at`, the sensitivity and the audience, because publishing is a recorded Admin act and sensitivity alone cannot say "indexed, not yet released".
