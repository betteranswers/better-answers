title: "A connected source is defined by origin, reach and destination, gated by three permission fields"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a connector, a connected source field or a media type's converter"
  - "Writing a read of passages, source entities or concepts that must apply the permission predicate"
  - "Changing how a connected source's or a document's sensitivity is narrowed, widened or published"
---

A connected source is defined by origin (company or external), reach (copied or read live) and destination (passage index, bundle, map), and gated by three permission fields, `published_at`, sensitivity and the audience, applied as one server-side predicate on every read. The first customer's knowledge arrives in four shapes at once, and one shape with two axes covers them all without a type per case. Publishing is a legal action with confirmations, and sensitivity alone cannot say "not yet" or "for one group only".

## Rejected

- Three axes or five flat source types: write is constant, so two axes are enough.
- Sensitivity as the only gate: it cannot say "indexed, not yet released".
