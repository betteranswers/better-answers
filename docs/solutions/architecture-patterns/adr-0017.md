status: accepted
title: "Every answer is a retained, correctable record; an Answer is minted only at the promotion gate"
applies_when:
  - "Recording an answer, the feedback on it or a correction to it"
  - "Minting or updating an `Answer` concept"
---

Every answer the platform gives is one retained record in _Questions asked_. Feedback and corrections are stored on that record. The record's skeleton is kept for good. Its content is kept for twelve months, in monthly partitions.

An `Answer` concept is minted or updated only at the promotion gate. An Editor proposes it. The owner of the `Answer` collection or an Admin decides each proposal, one at a time, beside the closest existing `Answer`.

Editors can feed the gate because a gate that only Admins can feed is a gate nobody feeds. A second person decides because without one, the wording of a single tender would become company knowledge.

## Rejected

- Thinning old content with `UPDATE`. It leaves dead tuples and holds locks, while dropping a partition costs nothing.
- Promotion as an _edit_ suggestion. A promotion has no target and no base hash until someone decides it.
