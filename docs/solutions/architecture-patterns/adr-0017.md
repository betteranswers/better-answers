---
status: accepted
title: "Every answer is a retained, correctable record; an Answer is minted only at the promotion gate"
applies_when:
  - "Recording an answer, the feedback on it or a correction to it"
  - "Minting or updating an `Answer` concept"
---

Every answer is one retained record in _Questions asked_ that holds its feedback and corrections. The skeleton is kept for good and the content for twelve months, in monthly partitions that leave by being dropped, never by a thinning `UPDATE`. An `Answer` concept is minted or updated only at the promotion gate, where an Editor proposes and the `Answer` collection's owner or an Admin decides each proposal beside the closest existing `Answer`, so one tender's wording never becomes company knowledge on one person's say.
