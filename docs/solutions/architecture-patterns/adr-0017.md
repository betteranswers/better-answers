---
status: accepted
title: "Every answer is a retained, correctable record; an Answer is minted only at the promotion gate"
applies_when:
  - "Recording an answer, the feedback on it or a correction to it"
  - "Minting or updating an `Answer` concept"
---

Every answer the platform gives is one retained record in _Questions asked_, its skeleton kept for good and its content kept twelve months by month partition, with feedback and corrections stored on it. An `Answer` concept is minted or updated only at the promotion gate, where the `Answer` collection's owner or an Admin decides an Editor's proposal one at a time beside the closest existing `Answer`. A gate only Admins can feed is a gate nobody feeds, and one with no second pair of eyes turns one tender's wording into company knowledge.

## Rejected

- Thinning by `UPDATE`: dead tuples and held locks, where dropping a partition is free.
- Promotion as an _edit_ suggestion: a promotion has no target and no base hash until decided.
