status: accepted
title: "Guides and write-ups are platform records that cite concepts and never restate them"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a guide, a section, a write-up or a response"
  - "Deciding whether a new key belongs in a concept file or in a platform record"
---

A guide definition and every write-up are platform records in Postgres, each with append-only versions. They cite concepts by IRI and never restate them. The platform also writes each guide's skeleton, which holds no prose, into the workspace repository at `guides/<guide id>.yaml` as a governed commit.

Guides are product features built over knowledge, and they are not knowledge themselves. Generated prose stored as a file drifts from the concepts under it. The skeleton file is there so that the structure of the map stays in the customer's repository and outlives the platform.

## The page

On the guide page, a section's Detail opens in place under its Brief. The Detail shows the included concepts in their own words. The reader stays on the guide page, so the Brief and the words it rests on are in view together. Each concept in the Detail links to its own page.

## Rejected

- Narrator concepts in the bundle. Their prose drifts, the bundle fills with state that belongs to one product's interface, and editing a guide becomes a git workflow for people.
- The database only, with no skeleton in the repository. That gives up a portable structure that no competitor offers.
