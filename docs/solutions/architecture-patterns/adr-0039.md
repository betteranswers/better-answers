---
status: accepted
title: "An audience is a word and a group-id array, combined by intersection and failing closed"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a readable unit, or a table whose rows a reader's audience must filter"
  - "Writing or changing the read predicate or the visibility derivation"
  - "Narrowing a connected source, or anything that re-derives a concept's or a write-up's visibility"
---

Every readable unit carries an `audience` column, _everyone_ or _groups_, and an `audience_groups text[]` column. One CHECK constraint ties the two columns together. The read predicate's term is `audience = 'everyone' OR audience_groups && $groups`.

Audiences combine by intersection, with _everyone_ as the identity. When an intersection is empty, the unit becomes Restricted, and the empty intersection is never stored.

A shape that means "nobody" today is the shape a bug writes tomorrow. The design makes a bug narrow an audience, never widen it.

## Rejected

- One nullable array, with NULL meaning everyone. The widest grant would be the absent value, so a row that a bug leaves unwritten would be visible to the whole workspace.
- Combining audiences by union. A concept minted from an HR document and a Sales document would be visible to more people than either document.
