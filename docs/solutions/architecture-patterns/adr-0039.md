status: accepted
title: "An audience is a word and a group-id array, combined by intersection and failing closed"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a readable unit, or a table whose rows a reader's audience must filter"
  - "Writing or changing the read predicate or the visibility derivation"
  - "Narrowing a connected source, or anything that re-derives a concept's or a write-up's visibility"
---

Every readable unit carries `audience` (`everyone` or `groups`) and `audience_groups text[]`, tied together by `AUDIENCE_CHECK`, and the read predicate's term is `audience = 'everyone' OR audience_groups && $groups`. Audiences combine by intersection, with _everyone_ as the identity. An empty intersection makes the unit Restricted and is never stored, so a bug narrows an audience and never widens it.

## Rejected

- One nullable array, with NULL meaning everyone. A row a bug leaves unwritten would be visible to the whole workspace.
- Union. A concept minted from an HR document and a Sales document would reach more people than either document.
