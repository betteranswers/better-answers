title: "An audience is a word and a group-id array, combined by intersection and failing closed"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a readable unit, or a table whose rows a reader's audience must filter"
  - "Writing or changing the read predicate or the visibility derivation"
  - "Narrowing a connected source, or anything that re-derives a concept's or a write-up's visibility"
---

Every readable unit carries `audience`, _everyone_ or _groups_, beside `audience_groups text[]`, tied by one CHECK, and the predicate's term is `audience = 'everyone' OR audience_groups && $groups`. Audiences combine by intersection with _everyone_ the identity, and an empty intersection forces the unit Restricted and is never stored. A shape that means nobody today is the shape a bug writes tomorrow, so a bug must narrow.

## Rejected

- One nullable array, NULL meaning everyone: the widest grant is the absent value, so a row a bug leaves unwritten is visible to the whole workspace.
- Combining by union: a concept minted from HR's document and Sales's would be wider than either piece of evidence.
