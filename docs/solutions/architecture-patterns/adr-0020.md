title: "Personal data is withheld at the redaction seam, and erasure rewrites what the platform wrote"
applies_when:
  - "Adding a step that reads a document's text before splitting, embedding, extraction or a model call"
  - "Changing a redaction rule, a descriptor, the window rule or what a finding row holds"
  - "Touching the erasure routine, a subject request, a suppression or the replay copy"
  - "Adding a store, a column or a copy that could hold a person's identifier"
  - "Sending a model text a person typed, such as a question or a note"
---

The redaction seam withholds personal data in the worker's conversion step and on the read-live tool's return, before splitting, embedding, extraction and every model call, so no derived store and no model ever holds the value, and the original bytes stay in the object store for an Admin alone, while text a person types never passes that seam and goes through the api's typed-text check, which withholds the always tier's patterns but not a name. The erasure routine rewrites every actor id the platform wrote, across files, history and rows other than the append-only audit log, to a per-workspace pseudonym, and a person edits what the company wrote. A placeholder written before splitting is the one control that holds whatever a predicate, a prompt or an assistant's context later does with the text.

## Rejected

- Quarantining flagged documents, or role-gating the passages: the useful file is the flagged one, and the value reaches every store.
- Rewriting on every leaver, or never rewriting: every clone diverges, or "we cannot" is not a basis.
- A targeted text replacement over concept bodies: it rewrites what the company asserts.
