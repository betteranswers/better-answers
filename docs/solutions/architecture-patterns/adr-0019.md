status: accepted
title: "Trust is derived from the file, told in fixed words, and moved only by a verification"
applies_when:
  - "Showing a concept's trust word or rider to a reader"
  - "Checking a concept, or computing or comparing its content hash"
  - "Writing, importing or erasing an actor id in a concept file"
---

Everything a reader is told about a concept's trust is derived from its OKF file by the spec's own rules, never stored, told in a closed set of words and moved only by a verification whose verifier differs from the generator. If the platform showed a word another consumer of the same file could not derive, the platform would disagree with the bundle it exports. The hash both tiers must agree on is a specification, RFC 8785 canonical JSON of the frontmatter less its trust keys plus the normalised body.

## Rejected

- The platform writing `stale_after` on every verification: policy dressed as knowledge.
- Opaque per-member ULIDs in the file: the email is the signal a reader of the raw file wants, and the erasure map carries the cost.
