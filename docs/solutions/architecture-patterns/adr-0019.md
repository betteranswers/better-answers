status: accepted
title: "Trust is derived from the file, told in fixed words, and moved only by a verification"
applies_when:
  - "Showing a concept's trust word or rider to a reader"
  - "Checking a concept, or computing or comparing its content hash"
---

The platform derives a concept's trust by OKF's rules from the file's `status`, `stale_after` and content hash and from the concept's latest verification, and tells it in a closed set of words (`trustWords` in `packages/core/src/concepts/trust.ts`). It stores no trust word, and only a verification, kept as a `concept_verification` row and imported from the file's `verified` events, moves the tier. Both tiers compute the content hash, so `contracts/concept-file/cases.json` fixes its exact form, SHA-256 over the canonical JSON of the frontmatter without its trust and identity keys, followed by the normalised body.

## Rejected

- Writing `stale_after` on every verification. That puts platform policy in the file as if it were knowledge.
