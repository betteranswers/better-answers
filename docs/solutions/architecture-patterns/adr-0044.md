status: accepted
title: "A passage's visibility is read from its connected source and its document, never copied onto its row"
applies_when:
  - "Writing a query that reads passages, or adding a column to index.passage"
  - "Publishing or narrowing a connected source or its documents"
  - "Changing the order of a sync's catalogue writes and its passage landing"
  - "Adding filter columns for a vector search over passages"
---

A passage's visibility is read at query time, not copied onto the passage's row. `index.readable_passage` is a `security_invoker` view. It joins each passage to its connected source and its document, and it takes the narrower of their two sensitivities. `index.passage` carries no visibility columns.

Before the view, five writers in two tiers copied four visibility columns onto each passage. A sync that read the connected source before a narrowing had committed landed its rows at the old, wider sensitivity.

## Rejected

- Copies that a database trigger keeps in step. The trigger would guard a mechanism that the view removes.
- A guard trigger that refuses a wrong value. Every writer would still compute the narrower sensitivity, and a race would become a failed sync.
