---
status: accepted
title: "A passage's visibility is read from its connected source and its document, never copied onto its row"
applies_when:
  - "Writing a query that reads passages, or adding a column to index.passage"
  - "Publishing or narrowing a connected source or its documents"
  - "Changing the order of a sync's catalogue writes and its passage landing"
  - "Adding filter columns for a vector search over passages"
---

`index.readable_passage`, a `security_invoker` view, joins each passage to its connected source and its document and takes the narrower of their sensitivities, and `index.passage` carries no visibility columns. When writers in both tiers copied visibility onto each passage, a sync that read the source before a narrowing committed landed its rows at the old, wider sensitivity. A trigger that kept the copies in step or refused a wrong value would still leave every writer computing the sensitivity.
