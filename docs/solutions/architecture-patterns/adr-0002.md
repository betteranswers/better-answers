status: accepted
title: "A concept is identified by an opaque platform-minted IRI, a bundle by its manifest"
applies_when:
  - "Minting, storing or reading a concept's IRI"
  - "Writing a link from one concept to another"
  - "Reading or adding a field of a bundle's manifest"
---

OKF identifies a concept only by its path and gives a bundle no identity, so the platform adds both. It mints each concept an IRI of the form `https://better-answers.com/c/<ulid>`, stored under the code-owned `iri` key, and identifies a bundle by `knowledge/manifest.yaml` at its root. A path would re-key verification history and every reference on each move, and an IRI built from a name would copy that name everywhere the IRI goes.
