Production is a Coolify Postgres resource and two Compose stacks on one box, `stores`, redeployed only for a store upgrade, and `platform`, redeployed on every release, because a Coolify redeploy restarts a whole stack. Every image is pinned by digest and migrations only go forward, so a rollback deploys the previous digest. Every irreplaceable byte is encrypted and copied off-host, and a restore replays the erasures completed after its dump, which is what makes it honest to call erased data "beyond use".

## Rejected

- WAL archiving from day one. It is a second process with its own failures, and it waits until the database passes 5 GB or a contract asks for a recovery point under an hour.
- A compliance-mode lock on backups for twelve months. A scripting mistake could never be fixed.
