Production is a Coolify Postgres resource and two Compose stacks on one box. The `stores` stack is redeployed only for a store upgrade. The `platform` stack is redeployed on every release. Every image is pinned by digest, and migrations are forward-only, so a rollback is a deploy of the previous digest. Every irreplaceable byte is encrypted and copied off-host.

A Coolify redeploy restarts the whole resource. With one stack, every release would restart the tunnel and every store.

Every dump is a copy of personal data. A restore therefore replays the erasures completed after the dump was taken. That replay is what makes it honest to say that erased data is "beyond use".

## Rejected

- One Compose stack for everything. Every release would cause an outage.
- WAL archiving from day one. It is a second process with its own failure modes. It waits until the database passes 5 GB or a contract asks for a recovery point under an hour.
- A compliance-mode lock on backups for twelve months. A scripting mistake could never be fixed, and "we cannot" reads as a failure to design for erasure.
