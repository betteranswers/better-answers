# V1 · Verification requests answered and conflicts resolved

**Destination.** A concept due for verification raises a verification request in its owner's queue, and an Editor or Admin verifies it. Only a verification moves a concept's trust. A reader sees shelf life and *Changed since verified* in fixed words.

**Done when.**

- A concept whose kind's verification interval has passed is listed on Knowledge › Due for verification and in its owner's weekly digest.
- Verifying the concept clears its request and changes its trust words, and a verification by the concept's generator is refused.
- A seeded conflict is resolved on Knowledge › Conflicts by supersede, deprecate, split by tier and dismiss, each writing its commit and audit event.

**Rests on.** ADRs 0003, 0012, 0017 and 0019.

**Builds on.**

- `packages/core/src/concepts/trust.ts`, which derives the trust words and both riders.
- The `concept_verification` table in `packages/schema/src/knowledge/concept-tables.ts`.
- The Clock in `packages/core/src/kernel/clock.ts`.
- A reader's flag on the answer record, stored by `give_feedback` in `packages/core/src/answering/feedback.ts`.
- Due for verification and Conflicts, declared but unbuilt in `apps/web/src/shared/navigation/areas.ts`.
- The owner record and Knowledge's pages, from S3.

**Must carry.**

- A verification whose producer part matches the concept's generator is refused.
- A verification needs view on every source the concept cites, or the page says *cannot check, source restricted*.
- One verification request is open per concept and reason, from the glossary's reasons. Each appears in its owner's queue and on Due for verification, and the interval's requests batch into the weekly digest. S5's routing turns a reader's flag into one.
- The verification interval is a per-kind workspace setting with platform defaults, never written into a file.
- Shelf life and *Changed since verified* are read at the Clock's instant, never an ambient one.
- The riders are derived. *· source moved on* comes from a check of the cited evidence. *· imported* marks an imported verification, and an actor alias lets *Verified by* name its member without rewriting the file.
- Each of the four resolutions is a governed write with one audit event. Supersession stays derived from the successor's citations, never a key.
- A sync raises conflicts from S7 on. Until then the resolutions are tested on a seeded conflict.
- The guide page shows each cited concept's trust badge and opens its evidence pane.

**Open for the plan.** Where the request, conflict and evidence-check records live, since no live ADR names their tables.

**Seams.**

| Seam | Proves |
| --- | --- |

| browser suite | Due for verification, Conflicts, the guide page's badge and the evidence pane |
