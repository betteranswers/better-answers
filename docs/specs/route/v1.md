# V1 · Verification requests answered and conflicts resolved

**Destination.** A concept due for verification raises a verification request in its owner's queue, and an Editor or an Admin who may view every cited source verifies it. Only a verification moves a concept's trust. A reader sees shelf life and *Changed since verified* in fixed words. A person resolves each conflict in one of four ways, and each is a governed write.

**Done when.**

- A concept whose kind's verification interval has passed is listed on Knowledge › Due for verification and in its owner's weekly digest.
- Verifying the concept clears its request and changes its trust words, and a verification by the concept's generator is refused.
- A seeded conflict is resolved on Knowledge › Conflicts by supersede, deprecate, split by tier and dismiss, each writing its commit and audit event.

**Rests on.** ADR 0019 for trust derived from the file, the riders and derived supersession. ADR 0003 for conflicts raised by the pipeline and resolved by a person. ADR 0017 for flags stored on the answer record and routed by S5. ADR 0012 for the governed write.
Words: *verify (a concept)*, *verification*, *verification request*, *verification interval*, *shelf life*, *Changed since verified*, *conflict*, *actor alias*, *evidence pane*, *feedback*, *owner (of a concept)*

**Builds on.** `packages/core/src/concepts/trust.ts`, which tells the trust words and both riders. The `concept_verification` table in `packages/schema/src/knowledge/concept-tables.ts`. The Clock in `packages/core/src/kernel/clock.ts`. A reader's flag stored on the answer record by `give_feedback` (#723), in `packages/core/src/answering/feedback.ts`. Due for verification and Conflicts declared in `apps/web/src/shared/navigation/areas.ts`, unbuilt. The owner record and Knowledge's pages from S3.

**Must carry.**

- **Verifier differs from generator.** A verification whose producer part matches the concept's generator is refused (ADR 0019).
- **View on every source.** A verification needs view on every source the concept cites, else the page says *cannot check, source restricted* (ADR 0019).
- **Verification requests.** One is open per concept and reason, with the reasons the glossary lists. Each appears in its owner's queue and on Due for verification. Requests raised by the interval are batched into the weekly digest. The request is the record that S5's routing turns a reader's flag into (ADR 0017).
- **The verification interval** is a per-kind workspace setting with platform defaults, and it is never written into a file.
- **Shelf life and Changed since verified** are read at the Clock's instant, never an ambient one.
- **The riders derived.** *· source moved on* comes from a check of the cited evidence. *· imported* marks an imported verification, and an actor alias lets *Verified by* name its member without rewriting the file.
- **The four resolutions.** Supersede, deprecate, split by tier and dismiss are each the governed write they make, with one audit event (ADR 0012). Supersession stays derived from the successor's citations, never a key (ADR 0019).
- **Conflicts before S7.** A conflict raised by a sync arrives with S7. Until then the resolutions are tested on a seeded conflict.
- **The guide page's trust.** The guide page shows each cited concept's trust badge and opens its evidence pane.

**Open for the plan.** Where the request, conflict and evidence-check records live, since no live ADR names their tables.
**Seams.** The core interface proves each request and resolution with its audit event and commit, and the shelf-life read at a fixed instant. The browser suite proves Due for verification, Conflicts, the guide page's badge and the evidence pane.
