# S5 · Every waiting suggestion decided on To decide

**Destination.** An Admin decides every waiting suggestion on Control Centre › Suggestions › To decide, item by item, or returns one to its proposer with a reason. An Editor saves an answer as an `Answer`, and the `Answer` collection's owner, from their Inbox, or an Admin decides it at the promotion gate. A reader's flag reaches someone's queue. One governed write reverts what a bad sync wrote.

**Done when.**

- The browser suite accepts, declines and returns items of one set on To decide, and each item shows its own result.
- A promotion accepted at the gate makes one commit with the decider as author, and an uncited one lands as `status: draft`.
- A flag of each reason lands in the queue *feedback* names.
- One forward commit reverts the concepts one sync wrote, rewriting no history.

**Rests on.** ADR 0012 supplies the suggestions, acceptance as the governed write, the replay and the forward revert. ADR 0017 supplies the gate's three actions, the trim rule and the uncited draft, and stores a flag before routing it. ADR 0015 turns include markers into `sources[]` footnotes at the gate.
Words: *suggestion*, *To decide*, *merge key*, *promotion*, *promotion gate*, *feedback*, *verification request*, *governed write*, *sync*, *watermark*, *head check*.

**Builds on.** `packages/core/src/concepts/suggestions.ts` submits, summarises, declines and returns. `acceptSuggestions` accepts item by item. `packages/core/src/answering/feedback.ts` stores flags. `packages/core/src/concepts/reconciler.ts` replays, and `apps/api/src/reconciler.ts` runs the head check. No tRPC procedure or page reads suggestions yet.

**Must carry.**

- **To decide.** The page lists every waiting suggestion with its payload, re-reads a set's summary on open, and shows each item's result and a returned item's reason. Nobody but an Admin or the set's proposer opens a set.
- **Save as an Answer.** An Editor raises a *promotion* from an answer.
- **The promotion gate.** The decider sees the proposed `Answer` beside the closest existing one, judged same, variant or different, with customer-specific wording to strip. The actions are update the existing, add as new and decline (ADR 0017). Include markers become `sources[]` footnotes (ADR 0015).
- **Flag routing.** A stored flag becomes a verification request, an edit suggestion, or an Admin's item. The verification request is V1's.
- **Corrections.** A correction records the level an answer went wrong at, and a retrieval correction is kept as an answer test (ADR 0017).
- **The by-sync revert.** Knowledge filters concepts by the sync that wrote them, and an Admin reverts them as one forward commit (ADR 0012). A suggestion set names no sync until S7's producer.
- **Replay findings, each a runtime test.** The replay stops and names the commit at each refusal: unreadable, rename refused, path taken, merge key taken, store error. A replayed creation whose derived merge key another concept holds keeps its own IRI as key. An up-to-date bundle reports no stop. A commit missing its `Actor:` or `Audit:` trailer is unreadable. A watermark outside the ref's history is history diverged. A lost commit that dropped or swapped a citation lands Restricted.
- **The head check's log.** A tick logs its five counts at warn on a stop or refusal, at info on a replay or held bundle, else at debug. A finished tick clears its in-flight mark, and `stop()` waits for the tick.
