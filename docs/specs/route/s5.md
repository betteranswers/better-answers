# S5 · Every waiting suggestion decided on To decide

**Destination.** An Admin decides every waiting suggestion on Control Centre › Suggestions › To decide, item by item, or returns one to its proposer with a reason. An Editor saves an answer as an `Answer`, which the `Answer` collection's owner decides from their Inbox, or an Admin at the promotion gate.

**Done when.**

- The browser suite accepts, declines and returns items of one set on To decide, and each item shows its own result.
- A promotion accepted at the gate makes one commit with the decider as author, and an uncited one lands as `status: draft`.
- A flag of each reason lands in the queue *feedback* names.
- One forward commit reverts the concepts one sync wrote, rewriting no history.

**Rests on.** ADRs 0012, 0015 and 0017.

**Builds on.**

- `packages/core/src/concepts/writes/suggestions.ts` submits, summarises, declines and returns. `acceptSuggestions` accepts item by item.
- `packages/core/src/answering/feedback.ts` stores flags.
- `packages/core/src/concepts/writes/reconciler.ts` replays, and `apps/api/src/timers/reconciler.ts` runs the head check.
- No tRPC procedure or page reads suggestions yet.

**Must carry.**

- To decide lists every waiting suggestion with its payload, re-reads a set's summary on open, and shows a returned item's reason. Only an Admin or the set's proposer opens a set.
- An Editor raises a *promotion* from an answer.
- At the promotion gate, the decider sees the proposed `Answer` beside the closest existing one, judged same, variant or different, with customer-specific wording to strip. The actions are update the existing, add as new, and decline. Include markers become `sources[]` footnotes.
- A stored flag becomes a verification request (V1's), an edit suggestion, or an Admin's item.
- A correction records the level an answer went wrong at, and a retrieval correction becomes an answer test.
- Knowledge filters concepts by the sync that wrote them, and an Admin reverts them as one forward commit. A suggestion set names no sync until S7's producer.
- Each replay finding is a runtime test. The replay stops and names the commit at each refusal: unreadable, rename refused, path taken, merge key taken, store error. A replayed creation whose derived merge key another concept holds keeps its own IRI as key. An up-to-date bundle reports no stop. A commit missing its `Actor:` or `Audit:` trailer is unreadable, a watermark outside the ref's history is history diverged, and a lost commit that dropped or swapped a citation lands Restricted.
- A head check tick logs its five counts at warn on a stop or refusal, at info on a replay or held bundle, and at debug otherwise. A finished tick clears its in-flight mark, and `stop()` waits for the tick.

**Open for the plan.** Whether a *decision model* matches a suggestion to a held concept, deciding same subject or new, and merge or new concept.
