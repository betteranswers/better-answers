# S8 · Concept vectors beside full-text entry points

**Destination.** A concept gains a vector, so `ask` finds an entry point whose words differ from the question's. `ask` embeds the question and fuses vector and full-text entry points.

**Done when.**

- An `Answer` an Editor writes gains its vector once a claimant runs its job, and `ask` returns it for a question that shares none of its words.
- The embedding model choice refuses a change while any vector exists (BA-16).
- Every embedding call writes one `llm_call` row. The worker writes none yet.
- The owner reads recall at ten on the first customer's answer tests again with vectors on.

**Rests on.** ADRs 0016, 0020, 0025, 0039, 0044 and 0049.

**Builds on.**

- The passage table in `packages/schema/src/sources/index-tables.ts`, whose `embedding` column is nullable and unwritten.
- The job kinds in `packages/schema/src/platform/job-tables.ts`.
- The model client in `packages/core/src/llm/`, whose sub-processor list names Mistral.
- The recall measure in `packages/core/test/answering/recall.test.ts`.

**Must carry.**

- The passage table gains a unit kind. A document passage keeps its connected source, and a concept passage has none, under one CHECK.
- The governed write inserts a catch-up job row in its own transaction, and the worker embeds each concept's latest version and writes its passages. Unlike every other kind, which is `enqueuedBy: "Admin"`, this kind admits an Editor's write. It joins the `queue` agreement under `contracts/`.
- A reader matches a concept passage only where the read predicate admits its concept, and a cascade on the concept reaches it.
- `readableClause` numbers its role parameter, and a vector parameter keeps the numbering right.
- The database refuses a change to the model choice, and the model choice's contract cases hold the refusal.
- An embeddings wire shape lands, with a fetch-shaped fake at the endpoint for the worker's tests and the cross-tier test.
- The DPIA input and the sub-processor list name the embedding provider from its first call.
- A concept just saved has no vector until a claimant runs its job, and the page says so.

**Open for the plan.**

- Read a concept passage's visibility through the readable view joined to the concept index, or carry the concept's columns for the cascade to rewrite.
- Weigh a decision model at the entry step beside the vector before building either, as the S2b plan (`docs/archive/plans/2026-10-11-2309-feat-s2b-ask-plan.md`) measures it.

**Trigger.** Either reading fires it, both under [The production readings of ask](../../operations/readings.md#the-production-readings-of-ask).

- Recall at ten on the first customer's answer tests, read in its workspace at C1, falls below 90 %.
- The owner judges the unmapped-passage rate's two counts too high. The rate has no threshold.

At the second customer's onboarding, the owner weighs the buyer-worded set again, under [Recall at ten on the synthetic sets](../../operations/readings.md#recall-at-ten-on-the-synthetic-sets).
