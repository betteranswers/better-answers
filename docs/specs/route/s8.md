# S8 · Concept vectors beside full-text entry points

**Destination.** A concept gains a vector, so `ask` finds an entry point whose words differ from the question's. The worker writes each vector on a job the governed write enqueues. `ask` embeds the question and fuses vector and full-text entry points.

**Done when.**

- An `Answer` an Editor writes gains its vector once a claimant runs its job, and `ask` returns it for a question that shares none of its words.
- The embedding model choice refuses a change while any vector exists (BA-16).
- Every embedding call writes one `llm_call` row. The worker writes none yet.
- The owner reads recall at ten on the first customer's answer tests again, with vectors on.

**Rests on.**

- ADR 0016, this block as the entry step's reserve, with its vector written on a job and never inline.
- ADR 0020, the embedding model choice fixed once vectors exist, and its provider in the DPIA input.
- ADR 0044, a passage's visibility read and never copied.
- ADR 0039, the cascade.
- ADR 0049, the model client's wire shapes.
- ADR 0025, one `llm_call` row per call.

**Words.** *concept index*, *passage*, *job*, *claimant*, *model choice*, *model client*, *wire shape*, *match*, *answer test*, *unmapped-passage rate*, *DPIA input*, *cascade*.

**Builds on.**

- The passage table in `packages/schema/src/sources/index-tables.ts`, whose `embedding` column is nullable and unwritten.
- The job kinds in `packages/schema/src/platform/job-tables.ts`.
- The model client in `packages/core/src/llm/`, whose sub-processor list names Mistral.
- The recall measure in `packages/core/test/answering/recall.test.ts`.

**Must carry.**

- **A unit kind on the passage table.** A document passage keeps its connected source, and a concept passage has none, under one CHECK.
- **The catch-up job kind.** The governed write inserts its job row in its own transaction. The worker embeds each concept's latest version and writes its passages. Every other job kind is `enqueuedBy: "Admin"`, and this kind admits an Editor's write. It joins the `queue` agreement under `contracts/`.
- **A concept passage's visibility.** A reader matches one only where the read predicate admits its concept, and a cascade on the concept reaches it.
- **The read predicate's placeholders.** `readableClause` numbers its role parameter, and a vector parameter keeps the numbering right.
- **The immutable model choice.** The database refuses the change (BA-16), and the model choice's contract cases hold the refusal.
- **An embeddings wire shape.** The worker's tests and the cross-tier test use a fetch-shaped fake at the embeddings endpoint.
- **The provider on record.** The DPIA input and the sub-processor list name the embedding provider from its first call.
- **The wait stated on the page.** A concept just saved has no vector until a claimant runs its job, and the page says so.

**Open for the plan.**

- Whether to read a concept passage's visibility through the readable view joined to the concept index, or to carry the concept's columns for the cascade to rewrite.
- A decision model at the entry step, weighed beside the vector before either is built, as the S2b plan measures it (`docs/archive/plans/2026-10-11-2309-feat-s2b-ask-plan.md`).

**Trigger.** The trigger fires when either reading holds. Both are under [The production readings of ask](../../operations/readings.md#the-production-readings-of-ask).

- Recall at ten on the first customer's answer tests, read in its workspace at C1, falls below 90 %.
- The owner judges the unmapped-passage rate's two counts too high. No threshold is set for that rate.

At the second customer's onboarding, the owner weighs the buyer-worded set again, under [Recall at ten on the synthetic sets](../../operations/readings.md#recall-at-ten-on-the-synthetic-sets).

**Seams.**

- The cross-tier document test, from a concept's commit through its job to `ask` finding it.
- The tier contract, for the job kind's conformance under `contracts/`.
- The worker harness, with the embeddings fake.
- Rebuild equivalence, naming the concept unit.
