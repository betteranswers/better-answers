## Goal

The operator's person sheet lists a grant the identity provider ended on its own, at sign-out or when the client revoked it, as an ended grant. It shows when it ended and says it was not an act of ours. Today such a grant simply drops off the sheet.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] `console.people.inspect` returns each grant whose standing refresh token the provider marked `revoked` as an ended grant. Its end instant is the mark's time, and its "ended by" says it was the provider's doing (the person signing out, or the client disconnecting), never a person or an act of ours. It sits among the ended grants, newest first.
- [ ] A grant appears once. If the provider marked it and one of our acts later deleted its rows, the sheet shows the provider's end, and the act's audit detail does not also record it as ended by the act. Each act's `grants` detail lists only grants that were still open when it ran.
- [x] The sheet reads such a grant as "Ended", with a by-line from the console's word table that names neither a person nor "the platform" (design readme §3). For example: "Ended when the person signed out or the client disconnected".
- [x] Core and api tests: a grant revoked at `/oauth2/revoke` by its client, and one revoked at sign-out, if Claude's grant can be (see Notes), each show as ended with the provider's by-line. Revoking everywhere after a provider revocation records the grant once. Each test is red against today's code first. The console-people browser spec shows a provider-ended grant.
- [x] `check` passes locally for core, api and web, and CI's `check` passes in the merge queue.

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Read the provider's own revocation marks as ended grants, and keep our acts from re-recording them.

**Current behaviour (verified on main 9fdf00b7, 27/09/2026):**
- `inspectPerson` builds open grants from the person's refresh tokens, taking each family's standing row with `revoked IS NULL`. It builds ended grants from the identity-set audit events of our acts, which T-460 added. Its type is `EndedGrantInspected`, with `endedAt` and `endedBy: AuditEventActor`.
- `@better-auth/oauth-provider` 1.7.5 still sets `revoked` itself in two cases:
  - at **sign-out**, on access tokens bound to the session, and on refresh tokens *without* `offline_access` (OIDC Back-Channel Logout §2.7);
  - at **`/oauth2/revoke`**, when the client revokes its refresh token.
- Such a grant is neither open nor in the audit log, so it vanishes from the sheet.
- T-460's acts record "what the delete removed". A marked row that one of our acts deletes later would therefore also be recorded as ended by that act.

**Decision (owner, 27/09/2026):** read the marked rows. Write no audit row and amend no ADR. ADR 0009 keeps a token's events as log lines. If the provider later deletes the family, for example on a replay of a revoked token, the grant leaves the sheet. That is accepted.

**Key interfaces:**
- The grants query in the workspaces people slice: split the standing rows into open (`revoked IS NULL`) and provider-ended (`revoked IS NOT NULL`).
- `EndedGrantInspected.endedBy`: widen it to carry "the provider" beside `AuditEventActor`, and follow it through the tRPC output and the console's person words.
- The three token-deleting acts' grant capture (`endingCredentials`, `endWorkspaceTokens`, removal): capture only rows that were still unrevoked.

**Out of scope:**
- Recording provider revocations in the audit log, or amending ADR 0009 or 0035.
- Changing the provider's sign-out or revoke behaviour.
- Telling sign-out and client revocation apart, unless the row shows it reliably. If it does, say so in the PR.
